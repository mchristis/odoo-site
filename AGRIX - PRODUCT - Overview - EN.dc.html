A system that continues after installation

Every module has a place, a serial number and a history.

Every eligible product is identified by QR/NFC. EMON records the as-built layout, module and string positions, serial numbers, commissioning, warranty, production, faults and replacements. When a module is changed, the project file changes with it.

From 50 kWp to multi-megawatt

Capacity is not the line between standard and custom.

AGRIX can begin with projects around 50–100 kWp. A small project can still require custom engineering when spans, soil, wind, snow, machinery, water or livestock conditions are unusual. The farm defines the geometry, not the other way round.

Manufacturing

AGRIX structures are configured around cultivation, machinery, soil and water requirements. Steel and aluminium components are formed, machined, finished and verified as TWIG canopy or HEDGE row assemblies. Module supports, cable protection and agricultural interfaces are supplied as identified project kits, with manufacturing records connected to installation and digital handover.
