Built for valuable crops

Berries, leafy vegetables, brassicas, fruiting vegetables, orchard rows and vines.

GB-2P is the principal AGRIX configuration where hail, heavy rain, sunburn or heat can reduce marketable quality. The design starts with the crop’s seasonal light response, then adjusts transparency, cell spacing, module gaps, roof pitch and open ridge width to distribute light rather than create permanently dark strips.
