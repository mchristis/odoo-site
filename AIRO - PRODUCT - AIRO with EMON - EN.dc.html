Time of use and dynamic tariffs

Fresh air at the cheapest hour.

EMON knows the electricity price for every hour: fixed day and night rates, or dynamic prices that change each hour. AIRO keeps the air fresh at all times, but EMON decides when the extra work happens. Boost ventilation, free cooling and the heating or cooling of GUST move to the hours when power is cheap or your own solar is producing.
