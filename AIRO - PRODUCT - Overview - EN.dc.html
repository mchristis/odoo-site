AIRO for…

Every building breathes differently.

HomesAIRO can manage the complete air cycle of the home. Bedroom: CO₂-controlled fresh air. Living room: occupancy-based ventilation. Bathroom: humidity extraction. Kitchen: background air exchange combined with appropriate local cooking extraction. Utility room: moisture management. Home office: fresh air during prolonged occupancy. 51
ApartmentsApartments can present a particular ventilation challenge: limited facade access, limited ceiling space, short duct routes, shared building structure, noise requirements and renovation restrictions. This makes both compact central systems and decentralised single-room ERVs useful depending on the project. 52
RenovationA complete duct system is not always practical in an existing house. That does not mean controlled ventilation is impossible. Through-wall single-room systems can provide a decentralised alternative, room by room or zone by zone, without rebuilding the entire house around ventilation ducts. 53
New buildA new building provides the opportunity to design ventilation into the architecture from the beginning. Central ERV. Supply ducts. Extract ducts. Zone control. CO₂ sensors. Humidity sensors. Filtration. EMON integration. Everything can be coordinated before construction. 54
SchoolsClassrooms combine high occupancy, long occupancy periods and rapidly changing occupancy. AIRO can use occupancy-related CO₂ demand to modulate ventilation according to actual classroom use. Ventilation should be designed together with occupancy, room volume, filtration, acoustic and local regulatory requirements. 55
OfficesThe office is no longer occupied the same way every day. One meeting room may remain empty for hours and then fill. Open-plan occupancy changes during the week. Demand-controlled ventilation is therefore especially relevant. Airflow follows occupancy rather than assuming every desk is occupied permanently. 56
HotelsHotel rooms have extremely variable occupancy: vacant, one guest, two guests, bathroom use, sleeping, away all day, return in evening. A decentralised or individually controlled ventilation system can adapt to room demand instead of treating every hotel room identically. 57
Commercial spacesRetail, clinics, small restaurants, gyms, meeting facilities, offices, education and shared workspaces all have different air-quality loads. AIRO should therefore be selected according to occupancy, floor area, pollution sources, required airflow, humidity generation, duct availability, noise limits, filtration needs and building regulations. 58

Manufacturing

Moulded housings, insulated air paths, recovery cores, filters and balanced fan assemblies are integrated with SMT controls and model-specific sensing. GUST adds a tested refrigeration circuit, while ROUTE provides verified distribution components. Finished units get electrical, airflow and functional checks before protected packing, with installed balancing recorded at commissioning.
