01 / How it works

One fan. Two directions. The heat stays inside.

PHASE 1 · EXTRACTWarm room air goes out.The reversible fan blows used room air out through the wall. On its way it passes the regenerator, a ceramic heat store, which absorbs its heat.
PHASE 2 · SUPPLYFresh air comes in, warmed.The fan reverses. Cold outdoor air is drawn in through the same regenerator, picks up the stored heat and enters the room close to room temperature.
PAIRED · BALANCEDTwo units work in opposite phase.While one unit extracts, the other supplies. Paired wirelessly, they keep the room balanced: as much air in as out.
