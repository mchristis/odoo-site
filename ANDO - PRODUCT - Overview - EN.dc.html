Flash test

Measured in the factory. Visible on site. Tracked for thirty years.

A flash test is a direct measurement of the electrical output of a solar module under controlled laboratory conditions — 1 000 W/m² irradiance, AM1.5 spectral distribution, 25 °C cell temperature (Standard Test Conditions). The result is a precise, measured electrical characterisation of that specific module — not an estimate, not a batch average.

Registration & traceability

One sheet. Every panel. The complete record.

Each ANDO panel carries two identical QR codes — a permanent label on the module rear and one detachable duplicate. During installation the duplicate is placed in its real position on the printed EMON scan sheet. A single scan of the completed sheet registers every panel at once — serial number, position, orientation, string diagram, inverter assignment and warranty — and builds the digital twin of the roof. No individual scanning. No manual data entry.

Manufacturing

Approved solar cells are inspected, matched and interconnected into strings. Robots position the strings within the module layers before controlled lamination. The modules are finished with frames and junction boxes, individually inspected and electrically tested, then robotically packed and traced through dispatch.
