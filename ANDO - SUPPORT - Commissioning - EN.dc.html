Flash test

Factory measurement.
Visible at installation.

A flash test is a direct electrical measurement of a solar module under controlled Standard Test Conditions — 1000 W/m² irradiance, AM1.5 spectral distribution, 25°C cell temperature. The measurement takes a fraction of a second. In that interval, the complete current-voltage curve of the module is recorded: current at every voltage point, the maximum power point, short-circuit current, open-circuit voltage, and fill factor.

Every ANDO module is flash-tested before dispatch. The result is stored against the module serial number — not against a batch, not against a production run. Against the individual unit. This record is linked to the QR code on the module and travels with it from the factory to the installation site.

The data displayed in the EMON app at the moment of installation scan includes the measured peak power, the short-circuit current, the open-circuit voltage, the fill factor, and confirmation of positive power tolerance for that specific unit. The date of the STC measurement at the factory is also shown.

The EMON degradation curve is initialised from the measured Pmax value, not the nominal specification. This means the 99% guarantee after year one and the 80% guarantee after 30 years are applied to the actual measured starting point — not the lower datasheet value. The monitoring is more accurate as a result.

No other consumer-accessible solar product delivers per-module flash test data automatically at the moment of installation via a standard phone scan. This closes the information chain from factory measurement to lifetime monitoring in a single action.

EMON smart registration

One sheet. Every panel. The complete record.

Each ANDO panel carries a QR code. During installation, that code is placed into a numbered window on the EMON scan sheet. A single scan of the complete sheet registers every panel — serial number, position, orientation, string diagram, inverter assignment, and warranty — in one action.

The main project QR opens the correct EMON project and approved layout. It does not replace scanning each panel identity.
