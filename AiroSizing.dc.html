How it is calculated
Airflow = the larger of people × airflow per person and room volume × air change rate. Ventilation heat loss = airflow × 0,34 Wh/(m³·K) × average indoor-outdoor difference × heating hours. Heat recovery saves that loss × recovery efficiency, valued at your heat price. Fan electricity = fan power × 8760 h × electricity price. Family suggestion: one room SHIFT; whole home without ducts SWAP; whole home with ducts FLOW; large room or commercial WAVE; air also heated, cooled or dehumidified GUST.
