Warranty

Two guarantees. Both in writing.

ANDO modules carry two separate warranties — a product warranty covering defects in materials and manufacturing, and a power output warranty guaranteeing minimum performance over time. Both are activated automatically at the moment of QR registration, with no additional paperwork required.

30 yr Product warranty

Standard product warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. If a panel fails due to a manufacturing fault, it is replaced. For Glas-Glas panels, the product warranty extends to 35 years — the dual glass layer provides additional protection against weathering and degradation.

30 years— Standard ANDO modules
35 years— Glas-Glas variant

If an issue occurs, contact the installer who placed the system. Strohm.energy provides direct support if the installer is not reachable.

30 yr Power output warranty

Every solar module degrades slowly over time. ANDO guarantees the rate at which this happens. Output is guaranteed at 99% after year one, and at 80% after 30 years. EMON tracks actual output against this guaranteed curve and flags deviations automatically.

EMON tracks actual vs guaranteed output from day one. If actual output falls below the guaranteed curve, the deviation is flagged and documented automatically.

EMON — performance monitoring

Your panel has a curve. You can see it.

Every solar panel loses a small amount of output every year. This is not a defect — it is the gradual effect of UV exposure, temperature cycling and time on the silicon cell structure. With ANDO and EMON, you see that curve for your specific panels, from year one.

That curve is not invented for the warranty document. It is calculated from real production data — cell technology, polysilicon lot, encapsulant batch, manufacturing parameters — and specific to the article code and production batch of your panels, not an average across thousands of different panel types.

Quality standards

Three standards that are not optional.

Every ANDO module is manufactured and tested to three performance standards that apply across the full range without exception. None of these are upgrades. None require a premium variant. They are the baseline.

Standard 01
Positive power tolerance
Every ANDO module is measured at STC before dispatch. The measured output is at or above the nominal rated power. No module ships below its nameplate value, confirmed by the flash test result linked to the serial number.
In practice this means yield calculations based on nominal power are conservative by default. Across a 40-module installation, the cumulative positive tolerance is a measurable contribution to actual yield from day one.
Standard 02
Low LCOE — Levelized Cost of Energy
The purchase price per watt-peak is not the cost of solar energy. LCOE is the total investment divided by the total electricity produced over the full system lifetime — the correct metric for evaluating a 30-year asset.

Standard 03
Triple tested — three points, per module
Every ANDO module passes three separate test points during production. This is not a sampling approach. It applies to every unit.

How — bifacial technology

Both faces active. Both measured. Both monitored.

A standard solar module generates electricity from the front face only. A bifacial module has active solar cells on both faces — the rear face captures reflected irradiance from the surface beneath the module and converts it into additional electricity.
