Tenders & RFPs

Most CHILL projects are procured by municipalities and public bodies through a public tender or RFP rather than a direct purchase. STRØHM.ENERGY provides the engineering and the products; installation is carried out by our EPC partners. For a bid, STRØHM.ENERGY can supply a company profile, reference projects, product certifications and an indicative technical and commercial proposal.

Manufacturing

Fabricated structures and public-use surfaces are combined with approved solar, storage and service modules. Mechanical interfaces, drainage, electrical protection and maintenance access are verified as one configuration. Finished assemblies are tested, identified and packed with manufacturing records linked to installation and public-asset handover.
