Purchase at factory price
Buy an agreed minimum quantity directly from STRØHM.ENERGY at a low, volume based price. Quantity, mix, terms and delivery are agreed before purchase.
02
Hold stock inside the EU
Products stay at your approved warehouse, stored correctly, insured where required, and visible in the STRØHM.ENERGY inventory system.
03
STRØHM.ENERGY generates orders
Installers order smaller quantities via the webshop, ERP or sales network. STRØHM.ENERGY selects the partner by availability, location, speed, region and rotation.
04
STRØHM.ENERGY buys units back
For each allocated order, STRØHM.ENERGY repurchases the units from you at the agreed higher price. This is a defined gross margin on every unit.
05
Payment before shipment
STRØHM.ENERGY pays you before goods are released. No credit to the installer, no waiting. Then you pack and ship per STRØHM.ENERGY instructions.
06
STRØHM.ENERGY handles admin
Installer ordering, invoicing, credit control, repurchase orders, allocation, serial registration, documentation, returns and communication.

Your stock stays yours until it is repurchased

You own the purchased inventory until STRØHM.ENERGY issues and pays a confirmed repurchase order. Your ERP remains the source of your stock data, and you control which approved products and quantities are available.

STRØHM.ENERGY does not guarantee that all stock is repurchased within a fixed period unless the agreement states it.

A defined margin on every unit

Prices and margins are set in the partner agreement. They are not published, and no income is guaranteed.

High MOQ purchase
You buy larger quantities at the lowest applicable volume price.
Low MOQ installer supply
Installers order single units, cartons, pallets or project quantities from EU stock.
Agreed margin
The gap between your purchase price and the STRØHM.ENERGY repurchase price, fixed in advance and never negotiated per installer.
