DEPOT | Stock Partner Programme
Proposed, to confirm

STRØHM.ENERGY manages the installer sale, pricing, allocation, invoicing, payment collection, documentation and communication. You provide secure local storage, accurate stock reporting and fast dispatch. Installers receive low MOQ European stock, and you do not build a sales and admin operation.

What DEPOT is not

Not a guaranteed return
You buy stock with your own capital before installer demand is confirmed. Sales volume, turnover and profit are not guaranteed.
Not a separate webshop
Installers order through STRØHM.ENERGY channels. DEPOT is the Stock Partner programme operating behind them.
Not exclusive by default
Territorial appointment does not create exclusivity unless the signed agreement states it.

Install it yourself, or distribute it

For your own installs
Draw from the same local stock for your own projects at your volume purchase price. No separate order, no waiting on central delivery.
For distribution
Release allocated units to installer orders routed by STRØHM.ENERGY and earn the agreed margin. One warehouse serves both your install business and your distribution income.
