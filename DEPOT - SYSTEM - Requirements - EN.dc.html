Live stock, so orders are confirmed with confidence

The API keeps your stock aligned with the STRØHM.ENERGY webshop and ERP. Updates run in real time where the connected system supports it, with retry and polling as fallback. Installers see validated stock rather than a delayed estimate.

No overselling or manual stock checks
Fast confirmation for installers
Automatic reservation when an order is allocated
Faster dispatch because the order is already validated

If the connection fails

Affected inventory is suspended from new orders until a reliable availability and reservation check has been restored.

Connection methods

Systems such as SAP, Microsoft Dynamics and Exact may be considered. Compatibility is assessed individually, and only verified connectors are labelled as supported.

Data protection

Your company retains control of its source inventory data. STRØHM.ENERGY uses only the data necessary for agreed trading and operational analysis, subject to contract and applicable law.

Each partner can access only its own data. Confidential prices and identities are restricted.

Stock and risk conditions

You own the purchased inventory until STRØHM.ENERGY issues and pays a confirmed repurchase order. STRØHM.ENERGY does not guarantee all stock is repurchased within a fixed period unless stated in the agreement.

The main commercial risk: capital is committed to inventory before installer demand is confirmed. Stock levels should be based on realistic regional demand, not optimistic forecasts. Every programme defines:

Approved product list
Initial MOQ
Maximum local stock level
Target stock rotation
Repurchase price
Programme duration
Product change protection
Slow moving stock procedure
Damaged stock responsibility
Returns and warranty process
Termination conditions

Selective regional access

The programme is selective. STRØHM.ENERGY may appoint a limited number of partners per country, region, product family or application, based on warehouse capability, financial capacity, logistics performance, market coverage and expected demand. Territorial appointment does not create exclusivity unless stated in the signed agreement.
