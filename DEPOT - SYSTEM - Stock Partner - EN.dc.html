When an installer needs a smaller quantity from European stock, STRØHM.ENERGY buys the required units back at a pre-agreed higher price. You are paid before you release the goods, and you ship directly to the installer.

STRØHM.ENERGY manages the installer sale, pricing, allocation, invoicing, payment collection, documentation and communication. You provide secure local storage, accurate stock reporting and fast dispatch, giving installers low-MOQ European stock without you building a sales and admin operation.

Two ways to use your stock

Install it yourself, or distribute it

For your own installs

Draw from the same local stock for your own projects at your volume purchase price. No separate order, no waiting on central delivery.

For distribution

Release allocated units to installer orders routed by STRØHM.ENERGY and earn the agreed margin. One warehouse serves both your install business and your distribution income.

The commercial model

A defined margin on every unit

You buy high-MOQ at the lowest volume price. Installers order low-MOQ from your stock. The difference between your purchase price and STRØHM.ENERGY's repurchase price is your margin: agreed in advance, never negotiated per installer.

Stock & risk conditions

You own the purchased inventory until STRØHM.ENERGY issues and pays a confirmed repurchase order. STRØHM.ENERGY does not guarantee all stock is repurchased within a fixed period unless stated in the agreement.

Selective regional access

The programme is selective. STRØHM.ENERGY may appoint a limited number of partners per country, region, product family or application, based on warehouse capability, financial capacity, logistics performance, market coverage and expected demand. Territorial appointment does not create exclusivity unless stated in the signed agreement.
