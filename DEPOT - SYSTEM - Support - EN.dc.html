QuestionAnswer
What is DEPOT?DEPOT is the STRØHM.ENERGY Stock Partner programme. Approved partners buy STRØHM.ENERGY stock in volume, hold it in EU warehouses and supply installers on STRØHM.ENERGY instructions.
Is DEPOT a separate webshop?No. Installers order through the STRØHM.ENERGY webshop, ERP or sales network. DEPOT operates behind those channels.
Who can apply?Registered professional businesses such as EPCs, installers, wholesalers and distributors with a suitable EU warehouse. Acceptance is not guaranteed.
Do I need to change my ERP?Not necessarily. An ERP system and API connection are required. Compatibility is assessed individually.
Can I use the stock for my own installs?Yes. You can draw from the same local stock at your volume purchase price.
Who sets the prices?Purchase and repurchase prices are agreed with you before you invest. They are not published and not negotiated per installer.
When am I paid?STRØHM.ENERGY pays you before goods are released for an allocated order. To confirm
What does joining cost?To be confirmed. Any onboarding or integration arrangements are discussed individually.
What if the ERP connection fails?Affected inventory is suspended from new orders until a reliable availability and reservation check has been restored.
How is my data protected?Each partner can access only its own data. Confidential prices and identities are restricted.
Can I leave the programme?Termination conditions are defined in the partner agreement. Confirmed orders remain binding.
Are sales guaranteed?No. Capital is committed to stock before installer demand is confirmed. Sales volume, turnover and profit cannot be guaranteed.
How do I apply?Select Apply as Stock Partner, choose Distributor / stock partner as your goal and complete the company, warehouse, ERP and financial details.
Apply as Stock Partner
