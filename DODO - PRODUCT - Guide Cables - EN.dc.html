Compatible cable types

The installer stays responsible for the electrical detail.

The system is intended for common solar DC cables, protective earthing conductors and compatible communication or optimiser wiring. The supplied UV-resistant ties can be used with common PV cable constructions, including 4 mm² and 6 mm² conductor sizes.

The installer remains responsible for cable diameter, bend radius, spacing, electrical separation and local standards.
