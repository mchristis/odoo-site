Why dirt bands form

Water evaporates and leaves the dirt behind.

Rainwater carries dust and fine debris down the glass. On framed modules, the raised lower edge can interrupt that movement. Water evaporates and leaves material behind, creating a persistent band close to the frame.

Aqua is not an optional cleaning accessory. One clip is supplied for every QUILL strip. It supports drainage and provides a secondary mechanical connection between QUILL and the panel frame.

What Aqua does not claim

Rainfall, roof pitch, local dust, pollen, salt and bird activity still affect panel cleanliness. Aqua helps reduce standing water and edge deposits, but it does not guarantee a maintenance-free solar installation.
