One edge system. Three essential functions.

A solar-panel edge is more than an open gap.

It is where birds gain access, where loose cables are often managed and where water can remain behind the aluminium frame. DODO combines these requirements in one system.

A better place to nest

Birds are not the problem.

The warm, narrow space beneath a solar array can look like shelter while creating noise, hygiene issues and avoidable risks for eggs and young birds.

DODO prevents access before a nest becomes active and helps homeowners provide a more suitable place in the garden.

Why prevention matters

The right moment is before nesting begins.

Once eggs or dependent young are present, access must not simply be closed. DODO therefore starts with inspection and local seasonal guidance.

Integrated product explainer

Designed as a system, not a loose collection of parts.

Every element has a defined role. Four factory-applied adhesive zones create the primary bond. The manual press tool applies controlled pressure. The Aqua clip provides secondary retention and a drainage path. Integrated eyes secure compatible cables. Male and female ends align adjoining strips.

Install faster

No panel-specific clip preparation for every fixing point.

QUILL arrives with four adhesive zones already applied. The installer works continuously along the panel edge, cuts around corners and obstructions, presses each zone for 10 seconds and adds one Aqua clip per strip.

Cable guidance built in

Cable control is built into the edge system.

Guidance eyes at the ends and middle of QUILL provide secure tie points for compatible solar DC cables, protective earthing conductors and other approved wiring — without adding a separate gutter along the array edge.

The supplied black UV-resistant cable ties accommodate common PV cable constructions, including 4 mm² and 6 mm² conductor cross-sections. Final routing remains subject to cable diameter, bend requirements and local electrical standards.

See the cable method
Improve drainage

Move water beyond the frame edge.

The raised aluminium frame can interrupt the natural path of rainwater and leave a damp dirt band at the lower edge of the glass. The DODO Aqua clip creates a route across the edge so water can leave more cleanly — and mechanically retains the strip as a secondary fixing.

Aqua helps reduce standing water and persistent edge soiling. It does not remove the need for inspection or cleaning in every environment.

See Aqua in action
Better habitat

Exclusion should be paired with a better alternative.

DODO prevents access to an unsuitable location, but the brand does not treat birds as pests. Every carton includes a regional biodiversity seed pack. After the installation is registered and photographs are uploaded, the customer can receive a DODO bird feeder or a suitable birdhouse.

Flowering plants support insects. Insects support many bird species. A correctly selected birdhouse can replace lost cavity habitat — but not every species uses the same house, opening size, height or location.

Responsible material

Recovered material. Engineered performance.

DODO aims to use the highest technically responsible proportion of verified recycled or ocean-bound plastic while retaining the flexibility, UV resistance and mechanical performance required at the solar-panel edge.

The verified percentage, source standard and test basis will be published once production material is locked. DODO does not use “cradle-to-cradle” wording without certification.

Manufacturing

QUILL strips are injection moulded in precision tooling and inspected for geometry, flexibility and fit. Aqua clips are stamped and formed from approved metal strip. Four adhesive zones are factory-applied under controlled conditions, then the components are counted and packed into complete installation cartons with batch traceability.
