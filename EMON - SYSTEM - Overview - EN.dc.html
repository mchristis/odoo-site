Commissioning · a main reason EMON exists

Scan. Connect. Validate. Document. Handover.

QR/NFC scan, Wi-Fi mesh discovery, automatic serial recognition, product mapping, a guided installer workflow, photo evidence, an automatic commissioning PDF, warranty activation and a permanent digital passport.

ANDO commissioning
Every panel verified, mapped and documented.

Scan module QR, register serials, attach flash-test data, record peak power, map roof position, assign string to MPPT, upload photos and activate the warranty: one permanent solar passport.

MOEBIUS commissioning
Inverter setup without scattered paperwork.

Scan the inverter, confirm firmware and grid settings, map MPPTs and strings, link battery and SYNQ meter, set export limits, run pass/fail checks and generate the report automatically.

Digital installation passport

One digital passport for the life of the system.

Every commissioned STRØHM.ENERGY system receives a permanent digital passport, product identities, serials, photos, firmware, flash-test records, test results, warranty and service history.

Access is controlled: owner, authorised installer and STRØHM.ENERGY support, transferable to a new owner or service company with permission controls.
