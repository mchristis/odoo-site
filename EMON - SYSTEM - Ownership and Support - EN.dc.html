EMON › OWNERSHIP AND SUPPORT

This page covers what you own, what STRØHM.ENERGY manages, and how to reach support.

Account and data

Your EMON account and its production, consumption and control data belong to you. STRØHM.ENERGY processes it to run monitoring, control and reporting, and to support your installer under the terms confirmed at commissioning.

Data retention periods, export options and third-party sharing terms are confirmed in the EMON terms of service — not yet published on this page.

Hardware warranty

Where EMON runs on physical hardware — a gateway, meter or EMON VISTA display — that hardware carries its own manufacturer warranty. Register it the same way as any other STRØHM.ENERGY product.

Support channels

Installers get a dedicated commissioning and diagnostics channel. Homeowners and businesses are supported through their installer first, with STRØHM.ENERGY support engaged for platform-level issues.
