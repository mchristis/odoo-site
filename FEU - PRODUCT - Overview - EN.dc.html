Dynamic & time-of-use tariffs

FEU runs only when energy is cheap.

On a dynamic or time-of-use tariff the electricity price changes through the day. EMON reads the price and switches FEU on only in the low-price window or the cheapest hours, and whenever your solar panels produce more than the house uses. Outside those moments FEU stays off and the main heating carries the house.

FEU with EMON

Extra comfort from energy that is already there.

DRAFT

FEU is not the main heating of the house. The heat pump or boiler does that job. EMON switches FEU on only when energy is plentiful or cheap: surplus from your own solar panels, the low-price window of a time-of-use tariff, or the cheapest hours of a dynamic tariff. The result is extra warmth where you are, without paying for it at the expensive moment.

Solar surplusWhen your ANDO panels produce more than the house uses, EMON turns that surplus into warmth in the bathroom, the office or on the terrace instead of exporting it for a low feed-in price.
Time-of-use tariffFEU runs in the low-price window, for example the night or midday rate. Outside that window it stays off.
Dynamic tariffEMON watches the hourly price and switches FEU on only in the cheapest hours, or when the price is negative.
Comfort, not base loadWarm bathroom tiles in the morning, a cosy corner in the evening, a heated terrace on a sunny spring afternoon. FEU adds comfort on top of the main heating.
Order of priorityEMON first covers the house, the battery and the heat pump. FEU gets the energy that is left over.
Your settingsYou choose the rooms, the comfort times and the price or solar level at which FEU may run. A manual boost is always possible.
Peak controlSeveral panels never all start at once, so the total power stays within the connection and any capacity-based grid fee.

How radiant heating works

Warm people and surfaces, not the air.

DRAFT
Like the sunInfrared panels send out radiant heat, the same kind of warmth you feel from the sun on a cool spring day. It warms the people, floors and walls it reaches, not the air in between.
Comfort at a lower air temperatureBecause walls and floors are warm, a room feels comfortable at a lower air temperature than with a convector or radiator.
No air movementRadiant heat does not stir up the air. Less dust circulates, which many people with allergies notice.
Dry wallsWarm wall surfaces stay above the dew point, which helps against condensation and mould in cold corners.
Fast responseA panel is warm within minutes, so a room can be heated only when it is used.
Silent and maintenance-freeNo fan, no water circuit, no moving parts.

[TO CONFIRM] Comfort and air-temperature effects depend on the room, insulation and panel position. No saving percentages are published until measured.
