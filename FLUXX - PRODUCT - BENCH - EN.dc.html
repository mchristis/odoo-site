In the box

EMON Smart Plug

Every BENCH comes with an EMON Smart Plug. Put it between the wall socket and an appliance, such as a washing machine, a dishwasher or a heater. It connects over Wi-Fi and appears in EMON next to your battery.

The battery decides when to store and when to supply. The plug lets EMON decide when an appliance runs, so more of your solar energy is used at home.

Questions

Good to know

What happens in a power cut?+

EMON keeps a reserve in the battery for power cuts. You choose how much in the app.

Can I use more than 800 W?+

Yes, if an installer makes a fixed connection. Otherwise EMON caps the output at 800 W where the law requires it.

Can I combine it with other OBJECTS?+

Yes. Each unit works on its own, so a second one goes on a separate circuit. EMON then runs them as one battery.

Do I need a smart meter?+

Only for zero export. With a SYNQ smart meter or meter reader, EMON delivers only what the home uses.

What if I move house?+

Unplug it and take it with you. Scan it again in the new home.

How long does it last?+

10 years or 6 000 cycles warranty with registration.

Every BENCH includes a 10-year warranty with registration. Full technical specifications are on the datasheet.
