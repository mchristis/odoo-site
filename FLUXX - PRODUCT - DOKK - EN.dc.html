What we make

A power station in a box

A DOKK container is a standard shipping container filled with batteries, cooling and safety systems. It arrives on a truck, is set on a foundation and connected to the grid.

Batteries use LFP, a long-life chemistry that does not overheat easily. A liquid cooling system keeps every cell at the same temperature, so the batteries age evenly.

Containers are sealed against rain, dust and salt air, and have fire safety built in. They are placed next to wind and solar parks, used to store cheap energy and sell it when prices are high, or to support the local grid.

Technical specifications per model are on the datasheets.

How it is built

From cell to container

Single battery cells are grouped into packs. Packs are stacked into racks, and the racks stand side by side in the container. Each step has its own monitoring, so a fault is found and isolated at the smallest level.

Example: FLUXX-5016-20HQ, 20 ft container with 12 racks.

Protection concept

Safety in four layers

Safety starts in the design and adds a layer at every level. If one layer is not enough, the next one takes over.

01DesignCoolant and electrical parts are kept in separate zones, so a leak can never reach live parts.
02Cell and packEvery cell temperature is watched continuously. A rise triggers an alarm, and each pack has its own suppression.
03ContainerHeat, smoke and gas detectors, ventilation with pressure relief, and automatic clean-agent extinguishing.
04ExternalA sprinkler network and an outside connection for the fire service.
