Runs on EMON

The battery decides for you

KOFR is controlled by EMON, the STRØHM.ENERGY energy app. EMON's AI learns how the site uses energy, reads tomorrow's prices and weather, and plans every hour of charging and discharging. You set the rules. EMON follows them.

01Peak shavingCovers short demand peaks from the battery, so the site stays under its contracted capacity.
02Dynamic tariff tradingCharges when power is cheap and uses or sells it when prices are high.
03Solar self-useStores solar energy from the roof instead of exporting it at a low price.
04Load forecastingPredicts the next day from past use, opening hours and weather.
05Grid congestion limitsKeeps import and export within the limits set by the grid operator.
06EV charging balancingShares available power between chargers and the building, so cars charge without tripping the connection.
07Backup reserveKeeps a set share of the battery ready for power cuts.
08Remote monitoring and serviceEvery module reports its state. Our service team sees faults early and often solves them remotely.
TO CONFIRM: backup reserve requires backup-capable power module or switchgear.

How it is built

Everything in one unit

KOFR holds the batteries, the inverter, cooling and safety in one weatherproof enclosure. There is no separate inverter to mount and wire. When the site needs more storage, a battery extension of the same size is placed alongside and connected. EMON adds it after one scan.

01Battery128 kWh inside the main unit.
02Inverter built in30, 50 or 80 kVA, 3-phase, connected at the factory.
03Air coolingKeeps batteries and inverter at a steady temperature.
04Battery extensionAdds 128 kWh per extension, air-cooled, same enclosure.

Which system

When KOFR is the right choice

KOFR is the simplest commercial system: one unit with battery and inverter inside. It suits sites that want storage without planning a separate inverter.

Choose KOFR whenThe site needs up to a few hundred kWh, has little room for separate equipment, and wants one unit to place and connect.
Why not SILOSILO holds more per cabinet, 144 to 241 kWh, but uses a separate inverter extension.
Why not KYUZKYUZ is the choice for flexible layouts and MW scale. Every stack and power module is placed separately.
Why not DOKKA container needs a crane, a prepared foundation and an EPC contractor. KOFR is placed by forklift.
