What it is

Furniture that stores energy

KUBUZ is a flat square battery that hangs on the wall like a picture frame. The main unit, KUBUZ INV, has the inverter inside and works with any existing solar system.

It installs with two connections. A backup socket keeps devices running during a power cut, and a programmable backlight turns the wall into soft ambient light in the evening.

The system

One main unit, as many colours as you like

Main unitKUBUZ INVBattery and bi-directional inverter in one. 2,56 kWh, up to 2,5 kW to the home and 2,5 kW backup output. Charges from solar or the grid and connects to EMON. Oyster White front (RAL 1013), Signal Black back (RAL 9004).
ExtensionKUBUZ EXTExtra 2,56 kWh battery, no inverter needed. Two cables to the main unit: a pre-assembled DC power cable and an RS485 data cable with waterproof connectors. Up to three extensions on one circuit, more than 10 kWh in total.

800 W mode in the app
KUBUZ delivers up to 2,5 kVA. Where plug-in batteries are limited to 800 W at the socket, switch on 800 W mode in the EMON app. EMON then caps the output to the legal limit for your country. Switch it off when an installer has made a fixed connection.

In the box

EMON Smart Plug

Every KUBUZ comes with an EMON Smart Plug. Put it between the wall socket and an appliance, such as a washing machine, a dishwasher or a heater. It connects over Wi-Fi and appears in EMON next to your battery.

The battery decides when to store and when to supply. The plug lets EMON decide when an appliance runs, so more of your solar energy is used at home.

Questions

Good to know

What happens in a power cut?+

The backup socket keeps one device running from the battery. EMON keeps a reserve for this.

Can I use more than 800 W?+

Yes, if an installer makes a fixed connection. Otherwise EMON caps the output at 800 W where the law requires it.

Can I add more storage later?+

Yes. Hang KUBUZ EXT extensions next to the main unit or in another room. No extra inverter. It also combines with other OBJECTS, and EMON runs everything as one.

Do I need a smart meter?+

Only for zero export. With a SYNQ smart meter or meter reader, EMON delivers only what the home uses.

What if I move house?+

Unplug it and take it with you. Scan it again in the new home.

How long does it last?+

10 years or 6 000 cycles warranty with registration.

Every KUBUZ includes a 10-year warranty with registration. Full technical specifications are on the datasheet.
