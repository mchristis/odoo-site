Runs on EMON

The battery decides for you

KYUZ is controlled by EMON, the STRØHM.ENERGY energy app. EMON's AI learns how the site uses energy, reads tomorrow's prices and weather, and plans every hour of charging and discharging. You set the rules. EMON follows them.

01Peak shavingCovers short demand peaks from the battery, so the site stays under its contracted capacity.
02Dynamic tariff tradingCharges when power is cheap and uses or sells it when prices are high.
03Solar self-useStores solar energy from the roof instead of exporting it at a low price.
04Load forecastingPredicts the next day from past use, opening hours and weather.
05Grid congestion limitsKeeps import and export within the limits set by the grid operator.
06EV charging balancingShares available power between chargers and the building, so cars charge without tripping the connection.
07Backup reserveKeeps a set share of the battery ready for power cuts.
08Remote monitoring and serviceEvery module reports its state. Our service team sees faults early and often solves them remotely.
TO CONFIRM: backup reserve requires backup-capable power module or switchgear.

How it is built

Start small, add later

Battery modules stack like building blocks. Stacks connect to power modules, and power modules run in parallel. There is no fixed upper limit: the same system scales from one stack to MW projects. When the site grows, add modules, stacks or power modules. EMON recognises the new parts after one scan.

01Power moduleHybrid inverter for solar, battery and building on a 3-phase connection. Several run in parallel for more power.
02BMS module and footThe base of each stack. Watches every module.
03Battery modulesStacked on the base until the stack has the capacity needed.
04CoverCloses the stack against weather and dust.
