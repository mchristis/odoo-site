Runs on EMON

Connected from the first plug

Every OBJECTS battery is connected to EMON, the STRØHM.ENERGY energy app. Limits, planning, backup and savings are set in one place.

800 W mode in the app
Every OBJECTS battery delivers up to 2,5 kVA. Where plug-in batteries are limited to 800 W at the socket, switch on 800 W mode in the EMON app. EMON then caps the output to the legal limit for your country. Switch it off when an installer has made a fixed connection.
