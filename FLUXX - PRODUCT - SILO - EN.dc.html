Runs on EMON

The battery decides for you

SILO is controlled by EMON, the STRØHM.ENERGY energy app. EMON's AI learns how the site uses energy, reads tomorrow's prices and weather, and plans every hour of charging and discharging. You set the rules. EMON follows them.

01Peak shavingCovers short demand peaks from the battery, so the site stays under its contracted capacity.
02Dynamic tariff tradingCharges when power is cheap and uses or sells it when prices are high.
03Solar self-useStores solar energy from the roof instead of exporting it at a low price.
04Load forecastingPredicts the next day from past use, opening hours and weather.
05Grid congestion limitsKeeps import and export within the limits set by the grid operator.
06EV charging balancingShares available power between chargers and the building, so cars charge without tripping the connection.
07Backup reserveKeeps a set share of the battery ready for power cuts.
08Remote monitoring and serviceEvery module reports its state. Our service team sees faults early and often solves them remotely.
TO CONFIRM: backup reserve requires backup-capable power module or switchgear.

Which system

When SILO is the right choice

SILO is a pre-assembled cabinet with 144 to 241 kWh. It suits sites that want a few hundred kWh in one unit, without many separate stacks or the crane and planning of a container.

Choose SILO whenThe site uses a lot of energy during the day, has space outdoors, and wants one installer to place and connect the system quickly.
Why not KOFRKOFR has the inverter built in and grows in 128 kWh steps. SILO holds more energy per cabinet and pairs with a separate inverter extension.
Why not KYUZKYUZ scales to MW, but every stack is placed and connected separately. SILO puts a few hundred kWh in one closed unit.
Why not DOKKA container needs a crane, a prepared foundation, an EPC contractor and weeks of planning. SILO arrives on a pallet.
