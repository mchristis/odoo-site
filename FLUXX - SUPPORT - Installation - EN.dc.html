Plug and play units need a short site check. Stackable, cabinet and container systems are installed by a qualified partner.

Contact a specialist Mounting per format

Four steps to a working system.

01
Site check
Location, floor or wall load, ventilation, distance to the meter and the solar inverter.
02
Mount
Wall bracket, floor base or anchors, as set for the model. Keep the service clearances free.
03
Connect
AC to the home, or DC to a paired inverter. Communication to EMON by cable or Wi-Fi, per model.
04
Commission
Register the battery in EMON, set the reserve and hand over to the owner.
