Compatibility

A battery-only product (BLOQ, STAQ) requires a documented, paired inverter — LV batteries pair with MOEBIUS ALVIX, HV batteries pair with MOEBIUS LUNOX or SYRA. Compatibility is confirmed by documented pairing; a matching voltage or physical fit alone doesn't confirm it.
