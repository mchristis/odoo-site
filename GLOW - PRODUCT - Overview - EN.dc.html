How solar lighting works

Charges by day. Lights by night. No cable to the grid.

Every GLOW light is its own small power plant: a solar module, a battery, a charge controller and an LED head. No trench, no grid connection, no electricity bill.

Inside a GLOW light

Four parts, one frame.

DRAFT
Solar moduleFour-sided or adjustable solar modules on the frame, sized for the light and the location. Example from the GLOW range: a four-sided module of 460 Wp with a battery of 1.152 Wh. TO CONFIRM per configuration.

Autonomy

Light on cloudy days, too.

DRAFT

A solar light is designed for the darkest weeks of the year, not for summer.

Nights of autonomyThe battery is sized to keep the light running for several nights with little or no sun.
LocationLatitude, orientation and shade from trees or buildings decide how much the module charges in winter.
Energy budgetSolar yield in the worst month has to cover the light used each night, with a margin.
Design per projectFor tenders, the light level, the burning hours and the autonomy are calculated per location.

Dimming and motion

Light when people are there.

DRAFT
Dimming profileFull light in busy evening hours, a lower level in the middle of the night.
Motion boostThe motion sensor raises the level for a moment when a cyclist or pedestrian passes, then returns to the dimmed level.
Longer autonomyDimming at night saves battery energy for the next cloudy days.
Less light pollutionLower levels at night are better for residents, insects, bats and birds.
Ecology modulesThe open module can carry a birdhouse, an insect hotel or a bee hotel instead of a light insert.

Light levels follow the applicable lighting class per road or path (EN 13201). Profiles per project: TO CONFIRM.

Manufacturing

Fabricated ARBOR frames are combined with solar assemblies, tested battery packs and SMT-produced lighting controls. CROWN and LANE heads use controlled LED, optical and thermal assemblies. Each configuration is programmed and functionally tested before protected packing, with its lighting profile and energy design carried through to commissioning.
