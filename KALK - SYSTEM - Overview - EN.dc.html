Public and professional mode

Public mode should favour a transparent range over false precision. Professional mode adds verified inputs and review, but a professional account alone does not make the result certified.

ModeInputs and outputHandover
PublicGuided input, disclosed defaults, approximate scenarios and a clear missing-data listOptional saved scenario or consented enquiry
ProfessionalVerified geometry and profiles, model-specific limits, documented assumptions and review workflowVersioned technical package and proposed BOM to KERN
Measured-data reassessmentPermissioned EMON historical data with quality and coverage flagsNew scenario revision; the original sales baseline remains preserved
InputsThe inputs used are shown with every result.
DefaultsDefault values and unknowns are disclosed.
LimitsLimitations and scope requiring professional review are stated.
Next stepThe result is explained before contact details are requested.

One project. One set of data.

A project created in KALK can be used across compatible calculators and configuration tools.

The site location, energy profile, selected products and technical assumptions remain connected to the project.

Changes to system capacity, component selection or layout trigger a recalculation of the affected results.

The objective is simple: avoid repeated data entry and keep engineering, financial analysis and proposal preparation consistent.

Built around the STRØHM.ENERGY product range.

KALK connects preliminary calculations to actual product specifications.

PV modules from ANDO and LINA. Inverters from MOEBIUS. Storage from FLUXX. Mounting systems from MOX. Other product families follow, as shown by their status.

Product selection is based on electrical ratings, mechanical dimensions, operating limits and compatibility requirements, not marketing descriptions.
