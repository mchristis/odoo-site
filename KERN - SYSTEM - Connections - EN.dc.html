Does every physical product connect directly to EMON?

No. Inverters, meters and other supported devices can exchange data. Mounting structures, protection products and other passive components are linked through installation and service records.

Can an installer access operational data indefinitely?

Only according to the permissions granted. Access should be reviewed and withdrawn when the authorised purpose ends.

How is automation different from AI?

A workflow follows defined triggers and conditions. AI interprets a request and can help retrieve, explain or prepare information. Both need permissions appropriate to the action.

Does AI have access to every record?

It should operate within the user’s authorised scope. AI is not a separate permission to view unrelated customers or confidential information.

Can AI control energy equipment?

KERN business AI does not provide blanket device-control authority. Supported operational functions belong to EMON and its separate permission model.

Which functions are available?

The proposal should name the available functions, pilot features and actions requiring review. Discuss the release scope before including AI in your implementation.
