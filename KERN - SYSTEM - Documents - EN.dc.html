Documents with a clear project reference

Connect drawings, proposals, survey reports, installation records and service documents to the work they describe. Help the team distinguish the current approved file from an earlier revision.

01
Identify purpose and status
Label the document type, revision and approval status. A draft engineering layout and an accepted installation drawing should not look equivalent in the working record.
02
Retain the history
Preserve the documents supporting earlier decisions and agreements. A revised scope should have a visible relationship to the version it replaces.
03
Share selectively
Give customers, subcontractors and other participants the documents needed for their role. Keep internal commercial material separate from the shared project record.
04
Prepare for service
Retain the as-built configuration, commissioning evidence and relevant equipment documentation so that a service team can work from the installed reality.
