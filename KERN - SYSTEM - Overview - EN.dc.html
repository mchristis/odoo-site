Manage work across the agreed company structure while keeping legal and commercial responsibility visible. Identify the entity, team and permissions attached to each transaction and project.

Assign the responsible entity
A quotation, purchase order and invoice must identify the appropriate company. Define the rules for entity selection before the team starts creating documents.
Retain each company’s terms
Keep the relevant document identity, payment conditions, financial configuration and commercial responsibilities attached to the issuing entity.
Define shared work
Where teams or resources serve several companies, specify what may be shared and who approves cross-company activity. Operational convenience should not obscure the transaction between the entities involved.
Separate external businesses
An unrelated installer’s workspace is not simply another internal company. Its customers, staff and commercial information require their own access boundary.

Questions about KERN

All questions in Knowledge
Who is KERN for?

KERN is intended for installers, EPCs and energy businesses managing equipment sales, installation projects and aftercare. It supports the business processes around solar, storage, heating, charging and other energy technologies.

Do we have to buy STRØHM.ENERGY equipment?

No. KERN is available independently. Product catalogues and connected services are configured around the equipment and interfaces validated for your implementation.

Do we need KALK and EMON?

No. They can be used separately. Connect KALK where technical design and calculations are needed, and EMON where supported operational data and energy management are part of the service.

Can we keep some existing systems?

Yes, subject to an agreed integration scope. During implementation, we identify which system owns each record and how information moves between them.
