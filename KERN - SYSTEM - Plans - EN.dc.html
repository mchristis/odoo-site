A proposal based on the work required

Define the users, workflows and services your business needs. Receive a proposal that separates the platform scope, implementation work and ongoing support.

Platform scope
Identify the modules, user roles, operating companies and locations included. Confirm any additional limits or paid functions before the agreement is accepted.
Implementation
Scope process review, configuration, migration, training and release work. Custom requirements and external connections should be described separately where they affect cost or delivery.
Ongoing services
State the support, maintenance and connection responsibilities included after launch. Additional work should have an agreed approval route.
Independent or connected
Use KERN independently or request a combined proposal with KALK and EMON. Each platform’s functions, access and commercial conditions remain identifiable within a bundle.

The proposal should identify which functions and usage conditions are included and which require an additional agreement.
