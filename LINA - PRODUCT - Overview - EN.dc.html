01 / The system

Solar panels and mounting.
Designed together.

A low vertical arrangement that brings energy generation, roof access and installation planning into one system.

LINA combines vertically positioned solar panels with an integrated support structure for rooftop installations. Its modular layout allows the solar array to be planned around the building, including roof edges, skylights, ventilation equipment, drainage and maintenance routes.

For conventional flat roofs and vegetated roofs, LINA offers a different way to arrange solar generation: low rows of upright panels, with the spaces between them treated as part of the roof design.

02 / Applications

Configure LINA
around your roof.

Conventional flat roofs
For buildings with membrane or other suitable flat roof constructions. The assessment considers roof condition, insulation, allowable loading and the compatibility of materials in contact with the roof surface. The support arrangement must protect the roof while transferring loads correctly.
Green roofs
For projects combining solar generation with rooftop vegetation. Panel clearance, plant height, substrate depth and access for vegetation maintenance are considered together. The solar layout should preserve access to drainage and allow the planted roof to be maintained throughout its life.
Gravel roofs and sites requiring additional clearance
Gravel, vegetation and local snow conditions can affect the required height beneath the panels. These applications need a specific review of the support configuration, surface condition and structural design.

Suitability depends on the building and the approved system configuration. Roof type alone does not establish compatibility.

03 / Energy generation

Consider when your roof
produces electricity.

A vertical bifacial array can produce a different daily generation profile from a conventional tilted array. With panel faces directed east and west, generation can be distributed towards the morning and afternoon. The actual profile depends on location, season, orientation and shading.

The hourly pattern matters
For a building with daytime activity, compare expected production with actual demand before deciding the array size or whether storage is useful.
Reflected light is part of the calculation
Bifacial panels can use light reaching either side, including light reflected from the roof and surrounding surfaces. Roof reflectivity, panel clearance and row spacing all influence this contribution. A light roof, a planted roof and a dark roof should not be assigned the same assumed yield.

Annual output should be estimated for the actual site and proposed layout. There is no universal percentage gain that applies to every roof.

Manufacturing

LINA combines ANDO panels with mounting parts from seven production lines: aluminium profiles (extrusion, sawing, cutting to length, drilling), steel profiles, clamps and brackets, toolmaking, plastic feet and clips, roof pads and surface treatment. Final assembly turns them into one roof unit with two, three or four panels.
