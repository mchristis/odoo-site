01 / Roof design

The roof remains
part of the system.

Weight and local pressure
Assess both the overall load on the building and the pressure beneath individual supports. Average kilograms per square metre do not describe the complete structural requirement. Roof insulation, support contact areas and any additional restraint or ballast also matter.
Wind and exposed locations
Building height, roof geometry, surrounding terrain and the position of the array affect wind loading. Any claim that a project needs no ballast or mechanical attachment must follow the assessment for that building.
Drainage and maintenance access
Keep drainage outlets, inspection points and equipment access clear. The layout should account for the tasks that will still be necessary after the solar installation is complete.
Existing roof condition
For a retrofit, assess the remaining service life of the roof before installation. Where refurbishment is approaching, coordinate roof works and solar installation as one project.

Assembly and commissioning are separate tasks
A simplified mechanical assembly does not remove the need for professional electrical work.
03 / Delivery

Plan delivery
before installation.

Confirm site access, unloading equipment, storage space and the method of moving the equipment onto the roof. Pallet dimensions and weights must be checked against the actual order. Do not assume that a roof can support concentrated pallet loads because it can support the distributed solar array.

04 / Unpacking and assembly

Pallets on site,
units on the roof.

Clearing the container
Pallets are removed from the container with a forklift. On stacked pallets, the gap between container and unit top can be as little as 8 cm — do not lift too high or the units may be damaged.
Loading and transport
Each pallet needs a minimum of 2 long straps fixed to truck hooks to prevent movement in transit. Disassembly of stacked or armored pallets requires qualified professionals.
No tools for the unit itself
The pre-assembled VPV unit integrates modules, cabling and mounting structure, with plug-and-play cable management. Assembly starts with the start beam and feet, then the first unit, then row by row.
Installation speed
Up to 10x faster than conventional panels, at approximately 15 minutes per kWp installed.
05 / Grounding and string sizing

Two checks
before commissioning.

CheckRequirement
GroundingConnect rows via the marked grounding holes at each beam end, using flat-head thread-rolling (not self-drilling) screws, max. 15 mm length. Avoid direct contact between dissimilar metals to prevent galvanic corrosion. All connections by qualified electricians.
String voltageString voltage = number of units × unit Voc. Voc rises as temperature falls (approx. -0.24 to -0.27%/°C) — size strings against the coldest expected temperature at the site, not standard test conditions.
MPPT allocationEach MPPT typically handles 2–3 strings, connected in parallel. All strings on the same MPPT must have the same number of units. Strings with different sun or orientation conditions should sit on separate MPPTs.
