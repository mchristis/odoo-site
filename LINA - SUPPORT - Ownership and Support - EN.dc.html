Routine inspection
Include the solar system in the building maintenance plan. Inspect panels, supports and visible cabling, keep roof drains accessible and check for vegetation or debris affecting the array.
Cleaning and vegetation management
A vertical arrangement does not eliminate the need for maintenance. Cleaning requirements depend on local conditions. Follow the product instructions and maintain vegetation so that it does not obstruct the panels, electrical components or required access routes.
