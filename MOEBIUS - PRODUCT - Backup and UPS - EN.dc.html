MOEBIUS Backup and UPS | STRØHM.ENERGY

A MOEBIUS HYBRID or OFFGRID system can create a separate backup supply — but only when correctly designed, sized and installed by a qualified professional.

Backup versus UPS
Backup

Restores or maintains power to selected circuits during an outage. A short transfer time is normal — equipment briefly loses power before backup takes over.

Suitable for lighting, refrigeration, pumps, heating controls and general essential loads.
UPS

Protects sensitive equipment with a very short or zero interruption. Whether a MOEBIUS configuration is UPS-suitable depends on transfer time, load type, earthing and neutral switching.

IT, communications and medical equipment must be assessed individually by a qualified professional.
What can be backed up

Backup can be configured for selected critical circuits or a complete distribution board, depending on the system design.

A system designed for occasional backup is different from one intended to run independently for several days. Critical and UPS-type installations must be designed and verified by a qualified electrical professional.

Generator integration

MOEBIUS OFFGRID and selected HYBRID configurations can work with a generator to support large loads, recharge the battery or reduce the required battery capacity. A poorly matched generator can run inefficiently or create unstable power.
