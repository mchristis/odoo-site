String, hybrid, backup, flexible-grid, off-grid — what the terms mean.

These describe different things and are often mixed up. String describes how panels connect. Hybrid describes solar-and-battery integration. Grid-conform, backup and off-grid describe how the inverter interacts with the public grid. Hybrid does not automatically mean off-grid — the available functions depend on the exact model.
