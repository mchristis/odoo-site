Boundaries, stated plainly

Backup is not the same as normal grid operation.

Backup and off-grid behaviour follow different rules to grid-connected operation. Capability depends on the model, battery and configuration, and is confirmed at commissioning — not assumed from a general claim. Supported-device and battery compatibility are limited to approved combinations.

Serviceability & software

Firmware that is recorded, not silently changed.

Automatic firmware keeps the fleet current, but every change is versioned against the commissioned record so an installer or owner can see what runs and when it changed. Serviceability is designed in — parts, access and diagnostics are part of the product, not an afterthought.
