Module-level safety

No high-voltage DC string across the roof.

PIXIE converts at the individual module. DC voltage stays limited to the connected module or module pair rather than combining many panels into one high-voltage string. At shutdown, AC output disconnects and each input returns to its local module voltage.

Export control & automatic phase detection

Measure once. Control the complete installation.

With EMON metering, PIXIE continuously adjusts production to the permitted export limit, coordinating microinverters over the low-latency mesh. At commissioning, EMON identifies the phase associated with each PIXIE group — the installer does not assign every unit manually, and the detected phase structure becomes part of the permanent record.
