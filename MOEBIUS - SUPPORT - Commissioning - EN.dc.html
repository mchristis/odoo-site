Every inverter installed. Every connection mapped. Every document ready.

Installing an inverter is only part of completing an energy system. The installer must record where equipment is located, how modules connect, which string belongs to which MPPT, how cables are routed and whether the finished system matches the design. MOEBIUS brings these steps into one structured commissioning process — across micro, string and hybrid inverters.

The method was previously distributed across overview, connection, product and warranty pages. It belongs in one place, because it is the product experience — not a support function added after the sale.

Identify

The inverter identifies itself— even unpowered.

A unique QR and passive NFC identity mean the installer never types a serial number. Tapping an unpowered inverter opens the exact product record; scanning the A4 project QR opens the approved project. The correct workflow starts automatically.

Standard Wi-Fi mesh

The system finds itself. You press one button.

Every MOEBIUS inverter forms a standard Wi-Fi mesh on site. There is no manual pairing, no typing addresses and no hunting for devices. The inverter appears in the commissioning app within seconds, supported devices join the mesh automatically, and the complete commissioned record uploads with a single tap — no laptop, no gateway hunt, far less time on the roof.
