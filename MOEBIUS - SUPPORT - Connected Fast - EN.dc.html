Integrated Wi-Fi Mesh

Every MOEBIUS inverter creates a network, not just a connection.

Most inverters connect individually to a router. When the signal is weak, the connection becomes unreliable. MOEBIUS creates a wireless communication network across the installation — compatible devices detect one another and relay data through the strongest route.

As more compatible devices are added, the network extends with them. The homeowner never configures it; the devices organise communication between themselves. Wi-Fi Mesh is built into the inverter — SYNQ is the separate hardware range that measures energy and feeds data to EMON.
