System design

The electrical design decides whether the hardware performs.

Battery communication, string sizing, DC oversizing and thermal placement determine real-world yield and safety. The inverter does not replace a properly engineered installation.

Battery integration

MOEBIUS HYBRID and OFFGRID communicate with compatible batteries over CAN or RS485. With FLUXX, inverter and battery are designed as one operating system. Third-party batteries may be supported, but must be confirmed for the exact model, BMS protocol and firmware.

Solar input & MPPT

Multiple trackers manage different roofs independently.

The MPPT continuously finds the operating point where the array delivers the most usable power. Separate MPPT inputs let east/west roofs, different string lengths or partial shading be handled independently. Correct string sizing remains essential — the installer must verify these limits.

Incorrect string sizing can reduce production or damage equipment. Temperature-adjusted open-circuit voltage must stay within the maximum DC input voltage.

DC oversizing

More DC than AC — up to a point.

A DC array larger than the AC rating raises annual yield: the array reaches the inverter's operating range earlier and stays productive later. But excessive oversizing causes clipping, thermal stress and warranty issues.

The correct DC-to-AC ratio depends on climate, orientation, angle, shading, input limits, electricity value, export limitation and battery capacity — and must stay within the inverter's electrical and warranty limits.

The trade-off
Benefit of oversizing
Higher morning/evening yield, better use of the inverter, improved economics.
Cost of too much
Clipping losses, higher thermal stress, input-current limits, warranty and compliance problems.
Thermal management & placement

Power electronics generate heat; if it is not managed, the inverter derates to protect itself. Installation location has a direct effect on performance — respect the required clearances.

Zero-export control

Where solar may not be exported, MOEBIUS uses SYNQ metering or compatible CTs at the grid connection point and adjusts output to prevent or limit export. Correct meter position, CT direction and communication are essential — an incorrectly installed CT can cause unstable control or unintended export.
