1. Style base

Architectural product photograph, anthracite powder-coated aluminium frame (RAL 7016), black glass-glass photovoltaic modules, clean European residential or commercial setting, soft overcast daylight, neutral colour grade, straight verticals, no people unless stated, no text, no logos, photorealistic, 35 to 50 mm lens

Avoid: night skylines, saturated skies, lens flare, CGI gloss, visible brand names, cluttered fair stands.
