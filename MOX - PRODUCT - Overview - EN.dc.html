The MOX position

MOX does not sell profiles, brackets and rails. It delivers a coordinated structure.

When a solar structure is assembled from a separate frame supplier, mounting rails, a drainage afterthought and site-tied cabling, responsibility for leaks, loads and serviceability becomes unclear. MOX designs the whole system as one product — so the structure is complete before the installer arrives with a drill.

Manufacturing

MOX structures are engineered for their application and site conditions. Custom extrusions and fabricated metal components are machined, finished and assembled with coordinated module supports, drainage, cable routes and service access. Verified components are identified and packed as project kits, with fabrication records carried through to installation and digital handover.
