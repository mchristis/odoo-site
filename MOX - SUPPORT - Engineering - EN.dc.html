Important engineering limitation

MOX can prepare project calculations, drawings and technical files according to the agreed scope. Local statutory approval, building permits, geotechnical validation and final sign-off by a locally authorised structural engineer may still be required. The exact responsibility must be defined before engineering begins.

Commissioning and record

Eligible MOX structures carry QR and NFC identity. At commissioning, configuration, components, tests and photographs are recorded against the project in EMON, and a PDF handover record is generated.

Engineering note
