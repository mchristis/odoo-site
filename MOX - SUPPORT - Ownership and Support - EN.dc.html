MOX › OWNERSHIP AND SUPPORT

Registration and periodic inspection keep the structural warranty in force.

Warranty

Review the structural warranty and the engineering certificate for the specific configuration ordered — load class, region and fixing method all affect coverage. Coatings and fasteners are covered separately from the primary structure.

Commissioning and record

Eligible MOX structures carry QR and NFC identity. At commissioning, configuration, components, tests and photographs are recorded against the project in EMON, and a PDF handover record is generated.

Engineering note

Values marked "to be confirmed" come from approved MOX engineering documentation and per-site studies. Final structural suitability depends on approved engineering for the site.
