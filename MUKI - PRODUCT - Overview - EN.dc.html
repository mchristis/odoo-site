Frequently asked questions

All questions in Knowledge

Read more: Frequently asked questions
What is MUKI?
MUKI is the brand for coordinated energy kits, preassembled modules and integrated systems.
What is ZORBA?
ZORBA is the integrated energy unit within the MUKI product architecture. Its configuration defines the electrical and thermal functions included.
Does every ZORBA include the same equipment?
No. Check the configuration-specific equipment list and technical documentation.
Are solar panels inside the unit?
Solar panels may form part of the overall package while being installed separately on the building.
Is an installer required?
Prepared connections can simplify installation. The site work, checks and commissioning specified for the configuration still apply.
Can ZORBA connect to existing equipment?
Compatibility must be assessed against the existing installation and selected configuration.
Can ZORBA be expanded?
Expansion depends on compatibility, available capacity, interfaces and installation conditions.
Does ZORBA provide backup power?
Backup operation must be explicitly included in the electrical design and configuration documentation.
Is KALK available?
The MUKI configuration calculation is planned and is not available yet.
Which controls does EMON support?
Available monitoring and control depend on connected devices, supported interfaces and commissioned functions.
