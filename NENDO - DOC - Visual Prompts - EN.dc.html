1. Style base for all product photos

Reference: nendo-outdoor-unit-01.jpg (Products hero). Every photo prompt below starts with this base.

Architectural product photograph, matte white enclosure with rounded edges and black vertical fins, small lowercase "nendo" wordmark, board-formed concrete wall with round tie holes, polished grey concrete floor, soft diffused daylight from the left, neutral greyscale palette, no people, no text other than the product wordmark, straight verticals, 50 mm lens, photorealistic, high detail

Avoid in every photo: saturated colour, lens flare, lifestyle props, people, logos other than nendo, visible cables unless stated, CGI gloss, fake reflections.

2. Rules for all app screens

Helvetica, greyscale only, radius frame and membrane-style buttons per the device rendering standard, simple line icons with consistent stroke width, realistic data (no round demo values like 50% or €100). Functional colour only where stated (orange for attention, green, orange and red status dots in the installer portal). File naming: NENDO_EMON_AppScreen_[Name]_v1.png. Format 9:16 phone, 4:3 for the installer portal tablet view.

7. EMON Functions page: app screens

Eight screens cover all twelve functions (plan from the EMON Function explainers, section 15). Build them as UI designs; generated images will not get text and data right. Page order: 13, 14, 16, 18, 19, then 20 in the installer section. Screens 15 and 17 go on the detail pages.

13 · NENDO_EMON_AppScreen_Home_v19:16 · Homeowner controls
Large room temperature with plus and minus buttons, zone tabs at the top, status line with explanation below. Cards: Hot water, Energy today, Plan. Settings: Comfort band, Preference (three-position selector), Surplus priorities, Household access. Frost protection card: shield line icon, status Standby or Active (orange when active).
14 · NENDO_EMON_AppScreen_TodaysPlan_v19:16 · AI layer, pre-heating, dynamic tariff
24-hour timeline with price bars (grey), solar forecast (light grey area), planned heat pump blocks (black) and battery state (dark grey line). Top card: "Plan saves €x today vs. unmanaged" only when measured baseline exists. Bottom: comfort band per zone. Label each stored-heat period, for example "Storing heat before 17:00 price peak". Cheapest hours in black, current hour marked.
15 · NENDO_EMON_AppScreen_Schedule_v19:16 · Heating scheduling
Weekly grid per zone, comfort blocks in dark grey with the target temperature label. Below, a toggle "Show EMON plan" overlays the planned NENDO operation as black bars. Override buttons: Boost 1 h, Hold until next block, Away mode.
16 · NENDO_EMON_AppScreen_SolarToHeat_v19:16 · Solar-surplus heating
Live energy flow diagram (PV, house, NENDO, battery, grid) with line icons and flow values in kW. Below, a daily bar chart: solar production (light grey), used by NENDO (black), exported (mid grey). Settings: surplus priority order (drag to reorder: Hot water, Buffer, Floor, Battery, EV).
17 · NENDO_EMON_AppScreen_Storage_v19:16 · Battery-aware operation
Two gauges side by side, battery state of charge (%) and thermal storage (buffer and DHW temperature vs. limits). Below, a 24-hour plan: battery charge and discharge (dark grey bars above and below zero), heat pump runtime (black), grid import (mid grey line). Setting: backup reserve (%).
18 · NENDO_EMON_AppScreen_HotWater_v19:16 · DHW scheduling, Legionella prevention
Tank icon with current temperature and estimated showers available. Timeline showing learned usage peaks (light grey) and planned heating runs (black). Buttons: Boost now, Holiday mode. Setting: minimum temperature. "Hot water hygiene" line: last cycle date, status Completed (no colour) or Rescheduled (orange), next planned cycle.
19 · NENDO_EMON_AppScreen_EnergyEfficiency_v19:16 · Consumption and COP monitoring
Segmented control Live / Day / Month / Year. Month view: stacked bar per day, heating (black), hot water (dark grey), other house loads (light grey). Right column: total kWh, total cost in euro, share from own solar (%), monthly peak (kW) with timestamp. Efficiency part: large "COP today" with label Measured or Estimated; scatter of daily COP against outdoor temperature, actual points black, expected curve mid grey; tabs Heating / Hot water / Total.
