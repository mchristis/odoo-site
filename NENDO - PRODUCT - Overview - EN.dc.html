The product range

Heat pumps, hot water and three extras.

CORE and AERO are the heat pumps. NENDO heat pump boilers make the hot water. The extras are added when the installation needs them: FLOW for the hydraulics, RAY to send solar power directly to the immersion heater in the tank, VESSEL for buffer and sanitary hot-water storage. EMON controls the system on dynamic tariffs. All products

EMON integration

Heating, managed as part of the energy system.

EMON turns NENDO into a flexible load. The AI forecasts heat demand, solar yield and prices 36 hours ahead, then plans every compressor hour. Comfort limits come first. Cost, peak and grid signals decide the rest. Homeowner controls are separated from installer-only technical settings. Safety-critical settings cannot be altered by users.

Manufacturing

Approved compressors, heat exchangers, hydraulic components and electronics are integrated into controlled mechanical assemblies. Refrigerant circuits are pressure tested, leak tested, evacuated and charged. Completed products are programmed, functionally tested and checked for system communication before protected packing and traceable dispatch.
