AERO

Air-to-water outdoor units

Vertical and flat-roof outdoor units, single- and three-phase, R290, MPPT-integrated.

AERO is an air-to-water heat pump, not a split system. The whole refrigerant circuit stays inside the outdoor unit. Only water pipes go into the house, so no refrigerant work is needed indoors. The warm water feeds the heating system and, through VESSEL DHW, the sanitary hot water.

Dynamic tariffs, solar and EMON

Why a heat pump belongs on a dynamic tariff.

On a dynamic tariff the electricity price changes every hour, following the wholesale market. Prices for the next day are published the day before. Octopus Agile in the UK works this way, as do dynamic contracts from suppliers in the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany. In the EU, larger suppliers must offer a dynamic price contract (Directive (EU) 2019/944, article 11).

DRAFT

Why heating can follow the price

A building stores heatWalls, floors and water in the system hold heat for hours. Heating can move to low-price hours without the room going cold.
Hot water can waitWith VESSEL, sanitary hot water is heated in the cheapest or sunniest hours and used later.
Low prices mean surplusPrices drop when wind and solar output is high. Running then uses power the grid has in excess.
Peaks can be avoidedDuring the highest-price hours the heat pump reduces output within the comfort limits you set.
Illustration. Results depend on the tariff, the building and how it is used.

How EMON plans it

1. PricesReads the next-day hourly prices from the energy supplier.
2. SolarReads the solar forecast and live production, including solar connected directly through RAY.
3. LimitsUses the comfort limits you set: room temperature range and when hot water must be ready.
4. PlanChooses the hours to heat, preheat, cool or charge VESSEL.
5. ControlStarts and adjusts the NENDO heat pump, and shows in the app what ran when.

Energy used in this order

1. SolarDirect solar surplus through RAY
2. BatteryStored energy from a FLUXX battery, where installed
3. Low-price gridThe cheapest hours on the dynamic tariff
4. Normal gridOnly when comfort needs it
