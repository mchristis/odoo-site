What is a heat pump boiler?

A heat pump boiler, also called a heat pump water heater, uses electricity to move heat rather than producing all of its heat directly from electricity.

A conventional electric boiler passes electricity through a resistance element: one unit of electrical energy produces approximately one unit of heat. A heat pump extracts low temperature heat from the surrounding air and upgrades it to a higher temperature, the same principle used by refrigerators and air conditioners, with the useful heat directed into the water tank.

Technology

Heat pump or electric element.

Electric elementTurns 1 kWh of electricity into 1 kWh of heat. Simple, low purchase cost, ideal for PV-direct heating with RAY.
Heat pump water heaterTakes heat from the air and delivers two to three times more heat than the electricity it uses. Lower running cost, higher purchase cost.
With solarBoth run on solar surplus. With a heat pump, the same surplus heats more water.
Which to chooseDepends on hot water use, available space and whether a NENDO heat pump already supplies the house.
