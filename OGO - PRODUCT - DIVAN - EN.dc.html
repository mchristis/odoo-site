Tap to view the water tank

Tap the NFC point with a compatible smartphone to open the vessel’s EMON dashboard.

The dashboard displays available water level, estimated remaining litres, temperature, pump status, pressure, measured consumption, irrigation settings and notifications. It also provides access to the product’s manual, maintenance information and service record.

Live readings require the monitoring system to be powered and connected. If current readings are unavailable, show the last update time clearly.

Reading the NFC tag does not automatically grant control. Pump, irrigation, lighting and any socket switching functions require appropriate user authorisation.

The NFC tag contains the product identification or dashboard link, not account credentials.

Read more: other pump arrangements

Choose the water-use arrangement

A low-pressure pump for filling a watering can is different from a pump selected for a long hose or pressurised irrigation circuit.

Specify the intended use and route length so that the pump and distribution components can be selected together. Do not assume that the base accessory pump can operate every irrigation system.

Two outlets

A hose outlet supplies watering and washing equipment. A separate filtered and pressure regulated outlet supplies drip irrigation. You select the application; the pump pressure is not changed by hand.

How the two outlets work

Lawn watering

Connect a compatible sprinkler to the supplied hose. The pressure system is designed around useful pressure and flow at the end of the hose. Compatibility will be specified for tested sprinkler models.

Watering duration depends on the sprinkler, available storage and any incoming rainwater. A larger reserve extends use but does not replace a properly sized irrigation plan.

Washing equipment

Use the spray gun for rinsing and the water fed brush for suitable surfaces. Water passes through the brush while you clean, with flow controlled by the selected accessory.

Follow surface care instructions, keep the brush clean and use appropriate water filtration. Stored rainwater is not automatically free of particles. This package provides ordinary washing and rinsing; it is not a high pressure cleaner.

Drip irrigation

The supplied drip starter kit delivers water near selected plants. Its separate filtered connection and pressure regulator match the supply to the included drippers.

Extend the layout only within the specified limits for line length, dripper count and total flow.

Cleaning and seasonal care

Read more: cleaning and winter preparation

Inspect the collection route and remove debris before it enters the reservoir. Use the specified service openings for internal access and follow the approved cleaning procedure.

Prepare vulnerable pumps, hoses, valves and fittings for freezing conditions. Temperature monitoring helps identify cold conditions but does not make the installation frost-proof.

How DIVAN is manufactured

DIVAN is made by STRØHM.ENERGY in its own mould.

Read more: material and processTo confirm

The DIVAN reservoir body is rotationally moulded from LDPE. Polyethylene powder is loaded into a closed metal mould, which is heated while it turns on two axes. The material coats the inside of the mould and forms a one-piece hollow body without welded seams. After cooling, the openings are finished and the service covers, inlet, outlet and connected equipment are fitted.
