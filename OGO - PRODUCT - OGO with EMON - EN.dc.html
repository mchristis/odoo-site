Connected system

The connected controller links the water level sensor, temperature sensor, flow meter, pressure sensor, pump control and irrigation valve. The pump itself does not need a separate Wi-Fi module.

Pump and water circuit

One branch supplies the garden hose, spray gun and water fed washing brush. A separate filtered branch reduces pressure to the level required by the drip irrigation system.

The vessel remains vented and unpressurised. Only the circuit downstream of the pump operates under pressure.

All connectivity and monitoring functions above are development requirements, subject to integration and validation.

Connection and local behaviour

The configuration identifies the controller, communication route, power source and local operating behaviour. If the remote connection is unavailable, the interface should show that state rather than imply that a command reached the equipment.

Confirm the actual behaviour of local schedules, manual controls and protection functions during commissioning.

Data and access

Clear data, clear permissions

Measured water delivery

Where a flow meter is included, review measured delivery for the circuit and period recorded. Without that meter, do not present estimated changes in tank volume as precise metered irrigation consumption.

Clear permissions

The owner manages operational access according to the supported permission structure. An installer’s temporary access should be limited to the purpose and duration granted.

An installation record, commercial invoice or equipment purchase does not by itself grant another party permission to start a pump.

Alerts and water quality

Critical alerts arrive at once. Other alerts are bundled.

EMON makes no claim about water quality. Stored rainwater is not drinking water.
