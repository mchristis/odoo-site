Use stored water appropriately

Stored water is not presented as drinking water. It can be used for irrigation, car washing, cleaning and other purposes, such as toilet flushing.

Water use ›

Select a system for the site

The right configuration depends on collection area, local rainfall, intended demand, available space and the pressure required at the point of use.

A tank volume alone does not establish irrigation performance. The pump, pipe route, filters, valves and connected equipment must work together.

Useful water, held locally

Keep a local reserve for suitable garden and outdoor uses. Rainwater stored in OGO can support watering and other approved non-potable tasks when water is available and the installation is configured for that use.

EMON helps you understand the connected reserve through tank readings and history. Pumping and automatic operation depend on the installed equipment, power and connection conditions.
