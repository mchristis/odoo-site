Use the appropriate outlet

A gravity outlet depends on the water level and the height difference to the point of use. A pumped system requires a pump suited to the route, pressure and delivery requirement.

Its smaller body does not include the integrated pressure pump, hose compartment or irrigation equipment package used with DIVAN and WALL.

A pump is available only as an optional accessory.

Add the selected sensing and control equipment as part of the connected OGO configuration. An ordinary tap is not remotely operated unless the required actuator or valve has been installed.

Inspect the collection and outlet components regularly. Follow the approved winter procedure for the tank, pump, valves and sensors.
