Two formats within one family

Slimline storage

Use a narrow reservoir arrangement along a suitable wall or boundary. Plan for the full tank depth, fittings, maintenance space and any required restraint.

Storage fencing

Use reservoir modules within a designed boundary arrangement. The installation must account for filled weight, supports, wind exposure, connections and access.

These formats have different construction and installation requirements. Capacity, dimensions and connection details are specified for each selected module rather than assumed across the family.

Equipment box

WALL separates the storage body from its watering equipment. A dedicated adjacent box accommodates the pump, hose and accessories, providing direct access for use and maintenance.

The separate box gives the hose and pump their own service space. Its position is selected together with the storage location, pipe route and available power supply.

WALL combines rainwater storage with an external equipment module.

The separate box accommodates the pressure pump, hose and irrigation equipment. It keeps the water equipment accessible without requiring it to be built into the storage body.

WALL provides the same equipment in a separate equipment box. Standard with both WALL formats: slimline storage and storage fencing.

Tap to view the water tank

Tap the NFC point with a compatible smartphone to open the vessel’s EMON dashboard.

The dashboard displays available water level, estimated remaining litres, temperature, pump status, pressure, measured consumption, irrigation settings and notifications. It also provides access to the product’s manual, maintenance information and service record.

Live readings require the monitoring system to be powered and connected. If current readings are unavailable, show the last update time clearly.

Reading the NFC tag does not automatically grant control. Pump, irrigation, lighting and any socket switching functions require appropriate user authorisation.

The NFC tag contains the product identification or dashboard link, not account credentials.

Plan the connected capacity

Select the number and type of reservoirs according to collection and demand. Added storage increases physical capacity; the usable reserve also depends on outlet position, operating limits and the intended use.

Where several modules are linked, define whether they balance hydraulically or operate as separately monitored tanks. A shared connection does not automatically provide an independent level reading for each module.

Installation requirements

Read more: installation requirements

Confirm the supporting surface, restraints, boundary arrangement, inlet heights and overflow route before selecting the module configuration.

WALL is not described as a wall-hung product unless the exact article is designed and approved for that installation. A storage panel does not become a structural retaining wall or security barrier merely because it resembles fencing.
