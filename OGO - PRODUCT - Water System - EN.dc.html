Pressure and flow

Pressure helps move water through a hose and operate a sprinkler. Flow describes how much water is delivered. A useful pump specification states both at the same operating point.

Maximum pressure and maximum flow are separate limits. They should not be read as performance available at the same time. OGO performance specifications will identify the tested hose, outlet conditions and water flow.

Drip watering

Drippers are normally specified in litres per hour. Total irrigation use is the sum of the connected drippers, multiplied by the watering duration.

Illustration For example, ten drippers rated at 2 litres per hour use 20 litres in one hour under their specified operating conditions. This is an illustration, not the final specification of the included OGO kit.

Use stored water appropriately

This range is intended for rainwater and non-potable applications such as suitable garden watering and configured water transfer. Stored water is not presented as drinking water.
