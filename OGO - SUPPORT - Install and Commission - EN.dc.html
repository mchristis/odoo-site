OGO›Installation
01

Confirm the location

Check dimensions, filled weight, support, restraints where required and access to the connections. Keep service openings, controls and drainage accessible after installation.

02

Define collection and overflow

Confirm the downpipe or other approved rainwater collection route, the correct inlet arrangement and the route taken by excess water when storage is full.

Overflow is a physical installation function. An app warning should not be its only safeguard.

03

Select the use circuit

Identify whether the installation fills watering cans, supplies a hose, serves irrigation or transfers water. Specify route length, elevation, pressure and flow requirements before selecting the pump.

04

Install measurement and control

Fit the approved sensors and controller with appropriate mounting, power and cable arrangements. Confirm each tank and circuit identity before linking it to the app.

05

Check the complete installation

Inspect for leaks, confirm inlet and overflow behaviour, calibrate the level-to-volume relationship and verify temperature readings and timestamps.

Test pump operation, manual stop, configured limits and any destination-high or source-low handling required for the selected circuits.

06

Complete the handover

Provide the final equipment list, installed configuration, operating instructions, permissions and cleaning and winter procedures. Make clear which features have been tested and which are not included.

DIVAN step by step

To confirmMeasurements and tools follow the approved DIVAN 750 L instructions.

Preparation

Unpack DIVAN and the connection parts. Remove the two rear service covers (Torx T20) and push the side plugs out from the inside. Keep the plugs for the winter.

Downpipe

Place DIVAN level on a firm, flat surface next to the downpipe. Mark the water level on the pipe, cut out the marked section and fit the diverter, sized to the pipe diameter. Connect the diverter to DIVAN with the supplied hose and angled connector, then check for leaks with water.

Pump

The pump module is factory-fitted in the equipment compartment. Connect the power cable to a weatherproof outdoor socket with RCD protection and connect the hose to the hose outlet. · Target

Read more: earlier pump step

The pump needs a 230 V outdoor socket. Fit the outlet coupling through the round opening with the seal on the outside, place the pump flat on the bottom of the reservoir and fit the hose connector. Route the cable through a grommet in the service cover and refit the covers.

Placement

Service clearance: To confirm. Keep the downpipe within 2 m of DIVAN. A filled DIVAN cannot be moved.
