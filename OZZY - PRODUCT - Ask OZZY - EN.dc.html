Installation

Some OZZY products plug in. Others belong in the installation.

Plug-and-play

NUBO, RUGO and OCTO connect to a suitable existing socket and commission through QR, NFC, Wi-Fi mesh and EMON — within the stated voltage, current and environmental limits.

Washing machines · dishwashers · office equipment · entertainment · outdoor lighting · small pumps. No change to fixed wiring.
Qualified electrician required

FLIP and other hardwired products are installed inside distribution boards and fixed circuits. Installation must be completed by a qualified electrician.

Heat pumps · boilers · EV circuits · pumps · motors · compressors · HVAC · commercial kitchens · high-starting-current equipment.
Install OZZY professionally.

Qualified electricians and energy professionals can register as STRØHM.ENERGY installation partners for technical data, EMON commissioning tools and, where eligible, local project introductions.
