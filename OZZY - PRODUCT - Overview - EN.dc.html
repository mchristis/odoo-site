Manufacturing

Precision-moulded housings, conductive contacts and SMT-assembled electronics are integrated into load-specific switching products. Every measurement channel is calibrated and verified, and each finished device undergoes switching, electrical and functional tests. QR and NFC identity connect the factory record to EMON commissioning before protected robotic packing and traceable dispatch.
