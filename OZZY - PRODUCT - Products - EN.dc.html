Every device extends the network.

DRAFT
How it worksEach OZZY device relays the signal of the next, so devices far from the router still connect.
SetupAdd devices one by one with the QR code or NFC tag. The mesh arranges itself.
If the router is downLocal schedules keep running. Remote control returns when the connection does.

Hardwired FLIP

Installed by an electrician.

DRAFT

Install & advice

Advice centrally. Installation locally.

Plug-in OZZY products — NUBO, RUGO and OCTO — are plug-and-play. Hardwired products — FLIP smart switches — require a qualified electrician. STRØHM.ENERGY advises and prepares the technical file; a local installer inspects, quotes, installs and certifies.
