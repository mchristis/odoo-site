How it is calculated
Showers and baths are counted as water at 40 °C and converted to hot water at the tank temperature. Kitchen and basin use is counted at tank temperature. Heating energy = litres × 4,186 kJ/(kg·K) × (tank temperature − cold water temperature) ÷ 3600, plus the standing loss of the tank. Tank size = daily hot water × 1,25, rounded up to the next standard size. Heating power = the energy to reheat a full tank in the chosen recovery time. PV-direct share = the part of the daily need that the solar surplus can cover, capped at 80 %. A heat pump water heater divides the electricity use by its COP. Surplus solar is valued at the feed-in price it would otherwise earn.
