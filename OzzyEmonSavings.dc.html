OZZY with EMON

The switch decides when. EMON decides why.

An OZZY plug or switch only turns power on and off. Connected to EMON, it switches at the right moment: when electricity is cheapest, when your own solar is producing, or outside the hours when your grid fee is highest. The appliance does the same work. It just does it at a better time.

Suited to loads that can wait: water heaters, dishwashers, washing machines, dryers, pool pumps, dehumidifiers, e-bike and tool chargers, and with FLIP larger fixed loads such as boilers and heat pumps. Not for fridges, freezers, medical devices or anything that must stay on.

How the saving is calculated
Energy per year = power × hours per day × 365. Time-of-use: the part of that energy moved from the peak price to the off-peak price. Dynamic tariff: moved from the average price to the average of the cheapest hours. Solar surplus: the share covered by your own solar is valued at the price you would have paid minus the feed-in price you no longer receive. Combined uses the larger of dynamic tariff and solar per kWh, not the sum. Payback = device price ÷ yearly saving. Installation cost saver compares a plug-in OZZY with a hardwired smart relay installed by an electrician.
