PACT provides campaign tools, registration tracking, communications and transparent progress reporting. Organiser benefits depend on published campaign conditions.

Start a collective energy purchase.

Bring together households, businesses or members of your organisation around a shared purchasing project.

Define who the campaign is for, which energy solutions it covers and where participants are located. PACT guides you through preparing the project, collecting interest and explaining how to proceed.

Each property may require a different installation. Collective purchasing does not mean that everyone receives the same system.

Submitting a project does not publish it, place an order or guarantee a discount. Campaign conditions must be reviewed and confirmed before launch.
