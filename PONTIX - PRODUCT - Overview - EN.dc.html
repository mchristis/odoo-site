Manufacturing

Hollow floats are blow moulded in qualified tooling, finished and individually leak tested. Connectors, module supports, walkways and project-engineered mooring interfaces are verified as connected assemblies. Components are identified and packed as project kits, with manufacturing records carried through to installation and handover.
