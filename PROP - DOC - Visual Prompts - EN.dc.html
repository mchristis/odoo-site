PROP visual prompts

Version 1 · 27 September 2026 · 12 core launch assets from the PROP website package

1. Visual direction

· Swiss/Braun discipline: white and restrained grey surfaces, strong black type and one PROP accent colour.
· No gradients or soft card shadows.
· Show realistic turbine scale, clearances and service access.
· Avoid luxury-home imagery with oversized turbines.
· Rotor motion must be slow and physically credible.
· Mark generated project scenes internally as visualisations until a comparable real project exists.
· Use diagrams for airflow, turbulence, swept area, power curves and systems.

2. Style base

Architectural documentary photograph, European site, soft overcast daylight, neutral greyscale grade, realistic turbine scale with clearances and service access visible, slow credible rotor, no gradients, no luxury-home staging, no logos except a small PROP wordmark
