Truth before product

THE TURBINE IS ONLY HALF THE PROJECT.

Wind output depends on the actual wind at hub height, nearby obstacles, turbulence, mounting height, terrain and the model-specific power curve. PROP checks these factors before recommending a turbine. When the location is weak, we say so.

PROP designs and supplies small wind-energy systems for locations where wind can make a measurable contribution. Every project begins with the site, not the turbine. PROP assesses local wind, obstacles, mounting height, structure, electrical architecture and intended use before recommending a system. PROP installations can operate alone or alongside solar, batteries and EMON energy management.

What PROP will not claim without evidence · "Silent" or "noiseless." · "Works in any wind direction" as a substitute for a measured power curve. · "Starts at 1.5 m/s" as evidence of meaningful annual production. · Annual kWh calculated from rated power alone. · Payback before site, tariff, maintenance and self-consumption inputs are validated. · Certification, lifetime, storm-survival or warranty claims copied from supplier sales material. · Rooftop suitability without structural, vibration and turbulence review.

Manufacturing

Approved blades, precision rotating components and tested generators are integrated with verified braking, supports and power electronics. Rotor geometry and balance are controlled, and each turbine gets defined mechanical, electrical and functional checks. Identified components are packed in supported transport fixtures, with manufacturing records carried through to installation and service.
