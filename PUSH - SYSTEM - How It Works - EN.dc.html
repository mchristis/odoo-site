Proposed, to confirm

From enquiry to completion

01
Customer enquiry
A customer requests information, a quotation or installation support through a STRØHM.ENERGY website or another approved lead source.
02
Lead qualification
PUSH collects and validates the information needed to evaluate the installation opportunity.
03
Installer matching
Suitable installers are selected according to location, installation category, qualifications, capacity and other applicable matching criteria.
04
Lead assignment
The enquiry is assigned to one suitable installer. A lead has at most one active installer at any time.
05
Follow-up and completion
Installers update lead status, contact the customer, provide quotations and record project outcomes.
