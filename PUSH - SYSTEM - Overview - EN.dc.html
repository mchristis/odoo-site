How PUSH works

STRØHM.ENERGY generates the enquiries. PUSH qualifies them, selects one suitable installer and monitors the result.

01
Customer Enquiry
STRØHM.ENERGY product websites and approved external sources send installation enquiries to PUSH.
02
Lead Qualification
PUSH qualifies and categorises each enquiry.
03
Exclusive Installer Matching
PUSH selects one suitable installer. A lead has at most one active installer at any time.
04
Installer Acceptance
The installer accepts or declines. Without a response in the defined period, PUSH may offer the lead to another suitable installer.
05
Quotation and Installation Follow-Up
After acceptance the installer contacts the customer, prepares a quotation and manages the opportunity. PUSH monitors the status.

What PUSH provides

Professional Installation Opportunities
Qualified customer enquiries from the STRØHM.ENERGY ecosystem.
Exclusive Lead Assignment
One active installer per lead. No competing installers on the same opportunity.
Geographical Matching
Leads are matched to installers by service area.
Technical Qualification
Installers are matched by the installation categories and qualifications they hold.
Lead Management
Offers, acceptance, contact, quotation and project status are recorded for each lead.
Performance Tracking
Response and outcome data is recorded per installer. No performance figures are published without recorded data.

A platform for installers

PUSH organises technical, commercial and project resources for professional installation companies in one place.

Business opportunities

Opportunities follow a defined workflow: intake, qualification, matching and acceptance. No quantity or revenue is promised.

Communication and growth

Communication and growth system for installer updates, campaigns and partner engagement.

Related platforms
