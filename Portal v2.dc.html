Account

Company details, delivery addresses, users and communication.

Who you are

Identity and contact details only. Everything commercial — payment terms, delivery terms, credit, currency — lives under Trading terms, because those change on a different clock and need their own history.

Where these details are used
Invoices, customs paperwork and warranty certificates all read from here. A wrong VAT number stops a shipment at the border, so changes to the tax fields are checked before they take effect.
Who can change what
Contact details are yours to edit. Legal name, VAT and registration number are verified fields — submit a change and we re-check it against the public register.
Photos
Add your own premises photo, or let us pull the street view and the images from your website. Drivers and forwarders see these, which is how a delivery finds the right gate the first time.

Trading terms

The commercial agreement behind every order you place: when you pay, who carries the risk in transit, and which currency the invoice is issued in.

Why this is separate from your company details
A phone number changes when someone changes desk. Payment terms change after a credit review, and both sides need to see when and why. These fields carry a history; contact details do not.
Delivery terms differ per route
Your headline incoterm applies to EU stock. Import and factory routes are quoted on their own terms, because the risk transfers in a different place. The route you pick at checkout shows which applies.

Who in your company can do what

One login per person, never a shared account. Each one carries its own permissions, so an engineer can check stock without seeing your margins and a junior can build a basket without being able to commit money.

The four controls
Purchase right decides whether they can place an order at all. Sees prices hides net pricing entirely. Spend limit caps a single order. Scope restricts them to certain brands or categories.
Approval instead of refusal
A user over their limit does not hit a wall — the order is submitted for approval and appears on the account owner’s Overview as something needing a decision.
Leavers
Deactivate a login and their order history stays intact and attributed. Nothing is orphaned when someone moves on.
