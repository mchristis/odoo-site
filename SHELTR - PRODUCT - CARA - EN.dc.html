What it is

CARA is a steel frame with a roof and integrated mesh that carries one or more inverters and protects them from direct sun, rain and animals. Wall types fix to a wall; floor types stand on the roof with ballast, without penetrating the waterproofing.

What problem it solves

Inverters on flat roofs are often mounted on improvised frames, exposed to direct sun, which reduces output through temperature derating and shortens service life. Birds and rodents nest behind equipment and damage cables. CARA provides a defined, shaded and protected position, sized for the inverter weight.

How it works
Frame and roofsteel, polyester solar-reflective powder coating, RAL 9006 White Aluminium.
Coatingexterior architectural grade, UV-stable, high Solar Reflectance Index, 60 to 80 μm dry film thickness.

Frequently asked questions
Does the ballasted version damage the roof?

Floor types stand on the roof without penetration. A protection mat under the feet is required; roof load capacity must be verified.

Why a reflective coating?

Inverters reduce output when hot. A high-SRI coating reflects solar radiation and keeps the shelter and the equipment cooler.

Can two inverters share one shelter?

Back-to-back types carry inverters on both sides. Wider sizes carry more units within the stated weight class.

Installed and documented

Each CARA installation is linked to its product and project record. The installer scans the identity, confirms the configuration and protected equipment, uploads photographs and completes the handover in EMON — activating the warranty and keeping a record for the equipment’s full life.
