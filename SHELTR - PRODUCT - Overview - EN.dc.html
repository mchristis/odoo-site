Installation & registration

Install it once. Keep the record for its complete life.

Each SHELTR installation is linked to its product and project record. The installer scans the identity, confirms the configuration, records the protected equipment, uploads photographs and completes the handover through EMON.

Biodiversity

Renewable energy should leave more room for life.

STRØHM.ENERGY manufactures renewable-energy products because nature has value in itself. Where it is technically safe, SHELTR can include or support a separate biodiversity module — kept completely outside ventilation openings, cable routes, drainage paths, electrical zones and service areas.

After a completed installation is registered with the required warranty photographs, the applicant can receive a free insect house. Permission to use project photographs is requested separately — never required for warranty or for receiving the insect house.

Manufacturing

Fabricated frames and panels are combined with application-specific weather protection, ventilation and service access. KUBO adds configured enclosure and thermal management components; HUSH adds validated acoustic treatment and airflow passages. Approved assemblies are inspected, identified and packed with their equipment compatibility and installation records.
