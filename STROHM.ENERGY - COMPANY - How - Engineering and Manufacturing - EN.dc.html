How

Every STRØHM.ENERGY product passes through the same sequence: engineering, production, testing and release. This page shows that sequence per product: the machines, the production steps, the tests and the checks before a product is released.

Product engineering

From requirement to validated product.

Before production starts, every product is defined, drawn, built as a prototype and validated. This explains steps 03 Engineer and 04 Validate from How in detail.

01

Requirement

What the product must do, where it is installed and which rules apply.

02

CAD and drawings

Components and their mechanical and electrical integration are modelled and drawn.

03

Prototype

Functional prototypes are built and tested on the bench and in the field.

04

Validation

Performance, safety and durability are checked against the requirements.

Traceability

Every product can be traced.

A number connects each product to its test results, from the production line to commissioning and service.

01

Tested

Each product gets its own serial or batch number. Test results are measured against it, for ANDO the flash test values and the EL image.

02

Released

When the product passes its final check, its number and all test results are designed to be uploaded to the STRØHM.ENERGY server.

03

Scanned

At commissioning, the installer scans the QR or NFC tag with the EMON app. Product type, serial number and firmware are filled in automatically, without typing.

04

Linked in EMON

The scan links the test record to the site and registers the warranty with installer, date and location in one step. EMON checks that the products on site work together and shows what is missing. For structures, it links batch number, material and structural calculation.

05

Serviced

Later, scanning the tag opens the product’s record, test results and maintenance history. Owners can see the test results of their own products in EMON.

These steps describe how traceability is designed to work. Availability is confirmed per product.
