← TECTIXEngineering and manufacturing

TECTIX roof-mount components are extruded, formed, machined and moulded to approved system drawings. Rails, attachments, clamps, roof protection and sealing components are verified for specified roof and module combinations, then packed as identified installation kits. Factory records stay connected to the fixing or ballast plan and the installed roof.

This is the proposed production framework for TECTIX flat-roof and pitched-roof mounting. Materials, dimensions, attachment details, roof loads and acceptance limits follow the approved specifications. Roof capacity, substrate condition and site friction remain installation engineering inputs.
