← TOGUEngineering and manufacturing

TOGU combines cable production, metal forming, polymer processing and electrical component manufacture with controlled harness and enclosure assembly. Products are inspected and tested to their own requirements, identified by article and batch, and packed as bulk supplies or coordinated project kits. Approved assemblies keep their factory test records through installation and service.

This is the production framework for the sixteen TOGU families. Materials, ratings and certification are specified per article. Compatibility is verified for the specified products, accessories and configurations.
