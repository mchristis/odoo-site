Who we are

One company for the complete energy system

We design and manufacture the hardware and software that generate, store, convert, distribute, manage and document energy.

STRØHM.ENERGY is a product company, not a catalogue of unrelated equipment. We design and manufacture specialised energy products to work together across seven systems, supported by one connected platform, consistent technical standards and support throughout the complete product lifecycle.

Responsibility should not disappear between suppliers.

STRØHM.ENERGY does not simply select unrelated products and place them in a catalogue. We define the product, industrial design, technical requirements, software behavior, documentation, commissioning process and support model.

We work directly with engineering teams and manufacturing partners to turn those decisions into physical products. Doing more ourselves requires more effort. It also gives us greater control over how products work together and how they are supported after delivery.
