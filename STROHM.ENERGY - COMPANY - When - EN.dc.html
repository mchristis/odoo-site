Manufacturing knowledge since 1988. Still questioning everything.

The professional experience behind STRØHM.ENERGY reaches back to 1988. Decades of experience in product development and manufacturing inform how we approach materials, production processes, technical details and product quality.

We combine that experience with contemporary engineering, digital technologies and new design thinking.

01Our story

STRØHM.ENERGY grew out of decades of manufacturing work. Its founders saw installers struggle with energy systems assembled from products that were never designed to work together.

The company set out to design the products, the connections between them and the software that manages them as one system.
