Where your data lives

Where does your data go, and who can access, update or control your energy system?

Connected inverters and batteries rely on digital services for monitoring, maintenance and software updates. These connections are useful, but access must be controlled and responsibilities must be clear.

EMON is designed to separate energy data from operational control, validate authorised commands and preserve essential local functions where supported by the connected equipment.

Data regions · not office locations

Your energy. Your data. Your control.

European UnionGermany

Primary hosting in Germany. EMON is designed so that EU customer data, backups and routine processing stay within the European Union, with international support access and subprocessors covered by technical and legal safeguards.

Our EU infrastructure keeps European customer data, backups and routine processing within the European Union. External access and subprocessors are subject to documented controls.

Hosting data in the EU does not by itself prevent unauthorised commands to a device. Communication between devices and EMON is intended to be documented, authenticated and controlled, including firmware, external connections and remote commands.

Essential local energy functions are intended to keep working during an internet or cloud interruption. Cloud services add monitoring, analytics and optimisation.
United KingdomUnited Kingdom

Separate UK hosting architecture for British customers, supporting regional data-handling preferences and applicable UK data protection requirements.
Asia-PacificSingapore

Singapore is the regional data location for selected Asian markets. Country-specific data protection, cross-border transfer and hosting requirements must be assessed before allocation. Hong Kong remains a commercial hub, not a data region.
Latin AmericaSão Paulo, Brazil

Regional hosting in São Paulo for Latin American markets.
AfricaVia European Union

Selected African markets are hosted through European infrastructure, subject to national data protection rules, transfer mechanisms and customer requirements.

Your data. No guesswork.

You deserve to know where your data lives.

Your energy data is valuable. You should know where it is stored, how it is processed, who can access it and which third-party services are involved.

Yet these details are not always easy to find or compare when choosing an energy system.

At STRØHM.ENERGY, we believe transparency should be standard, not an afterthought.

We aim to make our data practices clear and accessible, including where your data is stored, which services process it and how access to your system is managed. The applicable details depend on the product, deployment region and service configuration.

We have nothing to hide, and we believe you should never have to rely on guesswork when it comes to your data.

Questions about your data, privacy or system security? Talk to us.

We take these matters seriously and believe you deserve clear answers, not vague promises.
