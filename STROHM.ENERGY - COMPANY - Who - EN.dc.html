The right people. The right expertise.

At STRØHM.ENERGY, we believe that good products are only part of a successful energy project. What matters just as much is having access to the people who understand how those products are designed, manufactured, integrated, installed and supported.

That is why we connect installers, EPC contractors, distributors and project partners directly with the relevant people in our organisation.

Whether your question concerns a single product, a complete energy system, project engineering, an order or a local market, we want you to reach the right expertise without unnecessary layers in between.

Real people. Relevant expertise.

Not every question belongs with a salesperson, and not every technical issue belongs in a general support queue.

Read moreRead less

Our organisation brings together product designers, engineers, system specialists, project specialists, manufacturing teams and commercial professionals.

We make these different areas of expertise accessible so you can speak with the people who understand your particular requirements.

Close to installers and EPC partners

We develop our products with the people who specify, install and work with them in mind.

Read moreRead less

For installers, this means access to practical product knowledge, installation guidance, commissioning support and technical documentation.

For EPC contractors, it means support with system compatibility, engineering decisions, project requirements and technical coordination.

The objective is simple: fewer misunderstandings, faster decisions and less unnecessary work on site.

The right specialist at the right moment

A project rarely requires the same expertise from beginning to end.

Read moreRead less

Product specialists help with specifications and selection. System specialists address integration and compatibility. Project specialists support complex applications and engineering coordination.

Account managers help with quotations, orders and delivery. Local sales representatives understand their markets and maintain relationships with professional partners.

When additional expertise is needed, the relevant people can become involved.

One connected organisation

Product development, engineering, manufacturing, software, commercial operations and technical support should not operate as disconnected departments.

Read moreRead less

We work to keep these disciplines connected, so decisions made during product development remain understandable during installation, commissioning and long-term operation.

Products matter. The people behind them matter more.

A technically strong product can still become a difficult project when communication, documentation or support fails. STRØHM.ENERGY keeps product engineering, manufacturing, software, business development and technical support connected. This gives our partners direct access to the people who understand the decisions behind the product and can help move the project forward.
