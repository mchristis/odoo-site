Verdict

v2 is a genuine step forward and it is not a replacement for v1. It covers the web layer only, and it silently reverses three of v1's founding rules.

It closes the single worst v1 gap — the truncated brand hex codes — with a full 22-brand ramp, and it finally specifies a type scale, button states, and a spacing scale. But it drops the app system, the copy voice model, and the input states, and it introduces internal contradictions that will produce inconsistent builds until they are resolved. Treat it as v2 of the web layer, sitting on top of v1, and state that explicitly in the file.

Two real failures

Text / Placeholder at var(--color-grey-70) and Text / Disabled at #C8C7C3 both fall under 3:1 on white. Placeholders carry real instruction in the registration and filter forms, so this is not cosmetic. Darken placeholder to roughly var(--color-grey-70) and keep #C8C7C3 for disabled only, where the low contrast is the intended signal.

Thin weights at small sizes

The scale specifies Helvetica Neue Thin for 12px body on mobile and 12px minimum body on desktop. Thin at 12px is close to unreadable on a standard display, and it degrades badly on Windows. Set a floor: no Thin below 24px, and use Light or Regular for anything at or under 16px.

05 · What adoption costs in this project

The webshop, brand catalogues, and portal are built against v1 values, inline.
