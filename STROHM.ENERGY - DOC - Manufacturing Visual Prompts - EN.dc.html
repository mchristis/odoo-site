Style base

Industrial documentary photograph, clean modern European factory, neutral grey floors and white walls, even overhead LED light, calm organised workstations, operators in plain dark workwear seen from behind or cropped (no faces), no logos except the brand wordmark on products, no text overlays, neutral colour grade, 35 mm lens, shallow depth of field on the product, 4:3

Avoid: robots with sparks, blue glowing light, dirty or chaotic halls, staged smiles, Asian or European flags, visible supplier names.
