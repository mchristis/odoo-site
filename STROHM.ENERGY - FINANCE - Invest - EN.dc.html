How the platform works

Three parties. One quality gate. Zero projects wasted.

Project side

Installers and project developers submit energy projects they cannot or do not want to self-finance. Residential rooftops, commercial sites, community grids, battery installations.

STRØHM.ENERGY

Every submitted project goes through full due diligence. Technical study, yield modelling, legal structure, financial businesscase. Only validated projects move forward. No exceptions.

Investor side

Registered investors receive curated project proposals matched to their profile — ticket size, project type, geography, preferred return. No searching. Projects come to them.

Never lose a project

Never let a project die.

A client who cannot invest today is not a lost project. It is a project waiting for the right investor. Every time a client says no to the investment, that project has a second life — through STRØHM.ENERGY.

As a certified STRØHM.ENERGY installer you have one obligation when a client cannot or will not invest: pass it on. Not tomorrow. Now. STRØHM.ENERGY finds the investor. You still install. Everyone wins.
