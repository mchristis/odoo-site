How it works

Strohm.energy retains ownership of the hardware. You pay a fixed monthly fee that covers equipment, monitoring and maintenance. At the end of the term, you can renew, upgrade or purchase the equipment at its residual value.

Who it is for

Homeowners and businesses installing solar, storage or heat pump systems who prefer predictable monthly cost over upfront capital expenditure. Eligible products: ANDO, FLUXX, NENDO, MOEBIUS.
