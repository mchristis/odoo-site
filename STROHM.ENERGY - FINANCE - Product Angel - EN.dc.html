05 / Risk

Investment considerations.

Product development carries technical and commercial risk. Costs and timelines may change, a product may not reach production, and sales may not generate a profit.

Returns and repayment of invested capital are not guaranteed. Some or all of the investment may be lost. Review the project information and proposed agreement carefully before committing.
