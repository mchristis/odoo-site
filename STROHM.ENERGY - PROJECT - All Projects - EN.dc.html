We manufacture the products and help define how they become a project.

STRØHM.ENERGY is a product manufacturer and engineering partner. We do not present ourselves as the local installer for every project. We develop and supply the products, define how they work together and prepare the technical information required for reliable installation and commissioning.

Residential installations are completed by qualified local partners. Commercial projects may be reviewed and calculated by STRØHM.ENERGY before transfer to an installer or engineering partner. Utility and infrastructure projects can be developed as complete technical and supply packages.
