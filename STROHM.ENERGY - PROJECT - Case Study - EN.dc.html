Challenge

A newly built housing block of 14 units needed a shared rooftop solar array within a constrained roof surface, plus enough battery reserve to cover evening peak demand without exceeding grid import limits set by the local operator.

Solution

42 ANDO 440 Black panels mounted on MOX rail systems, paired with FLUXX battery storage and a MOEBIUS string inverter. EMON manages reserve thresholds across all 14 units from a single dashboard.
