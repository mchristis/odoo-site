02 · Made in-house

We design it. We build it. We test every unit.

Every STRØHM.ENERGY product is developed by our own engineers and produced on our controlled production lines. Each unit is tested before it leaves the factory and recorded under its serial number, with an NFC and QR code for follow-up on site, in service and over its lifetime.

04 · Product ecosystem
25 brands · one platform

Twenty-five brands. One product family.

Solar, storage, inverters, charging, heating, metering, mounting, controls and the infrastructure around them.

Each STRØHM.ENERGY brand focuses on a defined technical role. Together, the brands cover the principal equipment required to generate, store, convert, distribute, measure, control and use energy.

05 · System ecosystem
7 systems · one platform

Seven systems. Around every product.

Energy management, calculation, installer network, data core, notifications, contracts and logistics.

Each STRØHM.ENERGY system handles a defined part of the work around the products. Together, the systems cover how energy installations are planned, sold, supplied, installed, monitored and supported.
