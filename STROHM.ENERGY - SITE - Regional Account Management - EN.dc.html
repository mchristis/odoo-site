Role description: Regional Account Manager (internal draft)
INTERNAL DRAFT, NOT FOR PUBLIC DISPLAY. TO CONFIRM
Position

The Regional Account Manager is responsible for customer account management, webshop assistance, commercial administration and order coordination within an assigned European territory.

The role provides customers, installers, distributors and professional partners with a consistent point of contact throughout the commercial process.

Main Responsibilities
  • {{ d }}
    •
Technical Responsibilities

Regional Account Managers are expected to understand the STRØHM.ENERGY product portfolio at a general commercial level.

Detailed technical consultation, product engineering, system compatibility assessments and specialist application questions are handled by the relevant Product Experts.

The Account Manager remains responsible for communicating the outcome to the customer and ensuring the enquiry is properly closed.

Required Competencies

Professional communication, account management, webshop operations, CRM and ERP experience, organisational discipline and strong commercial administration skills.

Fluency in the relevant regional language or languages is required.

Reporting

Reports to the European Commercial Operations Manager.

Works closely with Product Experts, Logistics, Finance and Customer Support.

Core Principle

One accountable contact for every customer. Specialist expertise whenever required.
