Even before power-up
The NFC tag is passive, so product information, manuals and support routes can be reached while the device is switched off. Scanning always opens the workflow for the exact hardware installed. Live readings require EMON.

The installer only adds what is specific.

The device supplies its own identity. The installer adds the installation-specific details, such as the location, the circuit and the CT assignment, and never re-enters what the product already knows.

Digital installation record

Every installation documents itself.

During commissioning, EMON creates the digital installation record and links it permanently to that exact product.

The next installer arrives informed.

Circuit names, CT locations, meter assignments, phase and current ratings are recorded against the serial number. Service, extension or fault-finding starts from the installation as it is, not from a folder of unrelated manuals.
