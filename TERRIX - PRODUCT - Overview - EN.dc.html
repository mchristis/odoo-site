Manufacturing

Approved steel and aluminium components are formed, machined, finished and verified as fixed, tracking, deployable or vertical mounting assemblies. Tracker configurations add tested bearings, drives and controls; deployable systems add verified hinges, supports and locks. Identified project kits keep their manufacturing records through installation and commissioning.
