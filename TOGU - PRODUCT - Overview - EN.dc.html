Why quality matters

The cheapest part carries all the power.

DRAFT

Every kilowatt-hour from the panels passes through cables and connectors before it reaches the inverter, the battery or the grid. Undersized cable, a poor crimp or two connectors from different makers are small savings with large consequences.

LossesCable that is too thin for the current and the length turns part of the solar yield into heat, every day for 25 years.
HeatHigh resistance at a joint or a loose terminal warms up under load. Heat ages insulation and loosens the connection further.
Fire riskMost electrical fires in solar systems start at connectors and joints, not in panels. Cable fire class and correct installation limit the risk inside buildings.
Mismatched connectorsConnectors from different manufacturers may look alike but are not tested together. Only mate connectors of the same type and maker.
UV and weatherSolar cable is made for sun, rain, frost and ozone for decades. Ordinary installation cable is not.
DocumentationCable type, cross-section and fire class belong in the installation record, so an inspector or insurer can check them later.
