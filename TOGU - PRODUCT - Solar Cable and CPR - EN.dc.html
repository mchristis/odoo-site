CPR · reaction to fire

Cca and Dca describe how a cable behaves in a fire.

Under the EU Construction Products Regulation (CPR 305/2011), cable permanently installed in a building carries a Euroclass reaction-to-fire rating. For PV, the classes that matter are Cca and Dca. The letter is the fire performance; the lower-case additional classifications — s (smoke), d (flaming droplets), a (acidity) — describe secondary hazards.

Verify against project spec

Guidance only. National wiring rules, building class, fire strategy, cable route and the project specification determine the legally required class. TOGU does not reduce this to one generic rule — confirm with the inspecting authority or project engineer before ordering.

Dca
The common baseline for PV

Limited fire contribution, suitable for most standard residential and commercial rooftop routes. The default TOGU solar cable in markets that do not mandate a higher class.

Cca
The higher-performance class

Lower fire contribution than Dca. Often specified for escape routes, public buildings, higher-risk occupancies and some national requirements. Available as a TOGU Cca-classified solar cable.
