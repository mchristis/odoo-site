Value for public authorities

Voluntary citizen participation in clearly defined local projects.
Community-level visibility into selected performance indicators, with privacy protections.
Support for coordinated flexible demand and storage where technically supported.
Pilot-first deployment with measurable objectives and a decision gate for expansion.
Interfaces for independent operators, cooperatives and participating households.

Pilot proposal

Define geographical scope, representative buildings, DSO/network interface, meter eligibility, data permissions and commercial arrangements. Establish measurable baseline metrics, then compare pilot results with a transparent measurement and verification method.
