TRIBE connects eligible EMON-managed installations so households, buildings and local organisations can coordinate solar generation, storage, flexible consumption and community participation.

Start an energy communitySee how TRIBE works

One community. Independent installations.

Your home has its own energy profile. So does every home around you. TRIBE helps participating members coordinate those differences through a community platform while preserving individual installation control and the EMON account each member already uses.

What TRIBE enables

Community energy sharing
Support eligible arrangements through metering, allocation logic and transparent settlement records.
Collective load coordination
Coordinate available batteries, EV charging and other controllable loads to pursue agreed community objectives.
Grid flexibility
Where technically qualified and commercially authorised, enable aggregation through appropriate market partners.
Community insight
Report production, demand, shared allocations, peak demand and measured outcomes.

Built on EMON

EMON manages a site. TRIBE coordinates participating sites. A member can keep their own inverter, battery strategy, tariff preferences and device controls subject to their selected participation permissions.

Operational visibility

A coordinated system needs a clear view of energy flows, data and status. TRIBE treats transparency of flows and operational status as a basic requirement.

For every type of community

From a residential street to an apartment building, an energy cooperative or a municipal pilot, TRIBE provides a common framework for participation, coordination and reporting. The implementation depends on local market rules and infrastructure.

CO·OP and PACT

PACT helps groups purchase energy installations. CO·OP offers a proposed framework for jointly owned energy assets. TRIBE supports the operational coordination of participants and eligible shared assets. Each has a separate role.
