Why

Why we work only with a webshop for B2B buyers

We build, test and manufacture these products, and we have run real installation projects. That gives us a clear view of both sides — how a product must be prepared, and what an installer needs to finish a job without delays.

A classic wholesaler counter cannot carry that. Professional energy products need correct documents, clear stock routes, reliable logistics, warranty handling and structured reordering — connected to the manufacturer, not layered on top by a reseller.

So there is one route: a manufacturer-led webshop, open to registered buyers. Everything below is behind it. Nothing here is priced or orderable without an approved account.

How

How ordering works

Buyers choose which products to buy. As the manufacturer, we choose who represents them. Access is not automatic — it is applied for, selected, and agreed.

A two-way selection
We select who represents our products. You choose to stand behind our approach.

Our products carry our name onto your projects, so we are strict about who installs them. We ask installers to apply, then we run due diligence on the company and select one partner per exclusive area.

In return we train, teach and support you, and hand over qualified project leads through our lead generator. Strict conditions and correct agreements are the only route to a mutual success.
