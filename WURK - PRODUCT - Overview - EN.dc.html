Energy installers do not need more generic tools.

They need the correct tools for the work they repeat every day.

A connector tool must match the connector and cable. A tester must match the system voltage and measurement category. A battery trolley must match the product weight, dimensions, stairs and route through the building. Access equipment must match the height, duration, surface and load. WURK brings these decisions into one professional equipment system.

The WURK release standard

Safety equipment is not a colourway.

A harness, mobile tower, lifting trolley or powered access platform cannot be treated like a generic accessory.

WURK safety-critical products only move from development to public sale when the applicable technical file, conformity evidence, user instructions, product marking, traceability, inspection process and service responsibility are complete.

Where national rules, training or project engineering affect the selection, the website says so before a product can be ordered.

Manufacturing

WURK equipment follows product-specific routes, from precision metalworking and moulding to electronics, textile assembly and structural fabrication. Tools and instruments are verified for their declared tasks; safety, access and handling products are released with their technical evidence and inspection procedures. Identified products are assembled into compatible crew kits and supported through service and retirement.
