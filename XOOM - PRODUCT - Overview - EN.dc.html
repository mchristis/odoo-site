Manufacturing

Precision-moulded housings and fabricated supports are combined with SMT-assembled charging controls, metering and protected power circuits. Each charger is programmed, tested with vehicle simulation and verified for its supported EMON and OCPP functions. Approved units are packed with their installation accessories and traced through dispatch and commissioning.
